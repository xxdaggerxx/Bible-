# Hebrews
fits 1-13: A letter to Jewish Christians (author not named), before AD 70
1|3|God has spoken by his Son, who is the radiance of his glory and is greater than the angels.
2|18|Pay attention so we do not drift away; Jesus became like us to help those who are tempted.
3|13|Jesus is greater than Moses; do not harden your hearts as Israel did in the wilderness.
4|12|A Sabbath rest remains for God's people; the word of God is living and active; we have a high priest who sympathizes.
5|9|Jesus is high priest like Melchizedek; a warning to grow up in the faith.
6|19|Go on to maturity; God's promise is an anchor for the soul.
7|25|Jesus is a priest for ever, like Melchizedek, and always lives to intercede for us.
8|10|Jesus serves in the true tabernacle as mediator of a better, new covenant.
9|22|The old tabernacle pointed to Christ, who entered heaven with his own blood once for all.
10|14|By one sacrifice Christ has made perfect for ever those being made holy; hold fast and do not give up meeting together.
11|1|Faith is the assurance of things hoped for: the heroes of faith from Abel to the prophets.
12|2|Run the race looking to Jesus; God disciplines those he loves; you have come to Mount Zion.
13|8|Love one another, be content, remember your leaders; Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today and for ever.
