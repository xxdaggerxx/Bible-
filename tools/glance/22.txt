# Song of Solomon
fits 1-8: Wisdom: Solomon's song of love, about 950 BC
1|2|The bride longs for her beloved, the king, and they praise each other.
2|4|The lovers delight in each other; the beloved calls her to come away with him in springtime.
3|4|The bride searches for her love at night; Solomon's wedding procession.
4|7|The bridegroom praises the beauty of his bride.
5|16|The bride is slow to open, her beloved leaves, and she searches for him, describing his beauty.
6|3|The lovers are reunited: I am my beloved's, and my beloved is mine.
7|10|The bridegroom delights in his bride, and she gives herself to him.
8|6|Love is as strong as death; many waters cannot quench it.
