# 1 Kings
fits 1-11: Solomon's reign, about 970–930 BC
fits 12-16: The kingdom divides: Israel in the north, Judah in the south, about 930–874 BC
fits 17-22: Elijah and King Ahab, about 874–853 BC
1|30|As David grows old, Adonijah tries to make himself king, but David has Solomon anointed.
2|3|David charges Solomon to keep God's ways, and dies. Solomon secures his throne.
3|9|Solomon asks God for wisdom to rule, and God gives it, along with riches and honour. He judges between two mothers.
4|29|Solomon's officials, his wealth and his great wisdom.
5|5|Solomon prepares to build the temple with cedar from King Hiram of Tyre.
6|13|Solomon builds the temple in seven years. God promises to live among Israel if they obey.
7|51|Solomon builds his palace, and Hiram makes the bronze furnishings for the temple.
8|27|The ark is brought into the temple and God's glory fills it. Solomon prays and dedicates the temple.
9|4|God appears to Solomon again with a promise and a warning. Solomon's other buildings and trade.
10|9|The Queen of Sheba visits and praises Solomon's wisdom. His great riches.
11|4|Solomon's many foreign wives turn his heart to other gods. God will tear most of the kingdom from his son.
12|15|Rehoboam answers the people harshly, and the ten northern tribes break away under Jeroboam, who sets up golden calves.
13|2|A man of God from Judah prophesies against Jeroboam's altar, but disobeys God and dies.
14|9|The prophet Ahijah foretells judgment on Jeroboam's house. Rehoboam's reign in Judah.
15|11|Kings Abijam and Asa of Judah, and Nadab and Baasha of Israel. Asa does what is right.
16|30|A string of wicked kings in Israel ending with Ahab, who marries Jezebel and serves Baal.
17|16|Elijah announces a drought. Ravens feed him, and he stays with a widow whose flour and oil do not run out. He raises her son.
18|39|Elijah challenges the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel, and the LORD answers with fire. The rain returns.
19|12|Elijah flees from Jezebel in despair. God meets him in a gentle whisper and gives him Elisha.
20|28|God gives Ahab victory over the Arameans, but Ahab lets their king go.
21|19|Ahab and Jezebel have Naboth killed to take his vineyard. Elijah pronounces judgment, and Ahab humbles himself.
22|23|The prophet Micaiah foretells Ahab's defeat. Ahab dies in battle as the LORD said. Jehoshaphat's reign in Judah.
