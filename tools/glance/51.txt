# Colossians
fits 1-4: Paul's letter to the church in Colossae, written from prison, about AD 60–62
1|15|Christ is the image of the invisible God; all things were created through him and hold together in him.
2|9|In Christ the fullness of God dwells bodily; do not be taken captive by empty philosophy or rules.
3|2|Set your minds on things above; put on love; whatever you do, do it for the Lord.
4|2|Devote yourselves to prayer; let your speech be gracious; greetings.
