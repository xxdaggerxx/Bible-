# Titus
fits 1-3: Paul's letter to Titus, leading the churches on Crete, about AD 63–65
1|9|Appoint elders who are above reproach and hold firm to sound teaching.
2|11|Teach sound doctrine to every age; the grace of God has appeared, training us to live godly lives.
3|5|God saved us by his mercy, not by our works, through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit.
