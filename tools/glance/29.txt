# Joel
fits 1-3: Joel calls Judah to repent (date uncertain; traditionally about 835 BC)
1|15|A plague of locusts has stripped the land; Joel calls the people to mourn and cry to the LORD.
2|28|The day of the LORD is coming; return to me with all your heart. God will restore the years the locusts ate and pour out his Spirit on all people.
3|16|God will judge the nations in the valley of decision and dwell in Zion with his people.
