# Isaiah
fits 1-39: Isaiah prophesies to Judah before and during the Assyrian threat, about 740–700 BC
fits 40-55: Comfort for God's people, looking ahead to the return from Babylon and the Servant
fits 56-66: The future glory of Zion and the new heavens and new earth
1|18|God charges Judah with rebellion and empty worship; come, though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be white as snow.
2|4|In the last days all nations will come to God's mountain, and swords will be beaten into ploughshares.
3|10|Judah's leaders and proud women will face judgment.
4|2|After judgment, the Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and Zion will be holy.
5|7|The song of the vineyard: God's people produced bad fruit. Woe to the greedy and the proud.
6|8|Isaiah sees the LORD on his throne, is cleansed, and answers: Here am I, send me.
7|14|King Ahaz will not trust God; the sign: a virgin will conceive and bear a son, Immanuel.
8|13|Assyria is coming; fear the LORD, who is a sanctuary to those who trust him.
9|6|A light dawns: a child is born, Wonderful Counsellor, Mighty God, Prince of Peace.
10|20|Assyria is the rod of God's anger, but will itself be judged; a remnant will return.
11|1|A shoot from Jesse's stump: the Spirit-filled King will bring justice and peace, and the wolf will live with the lamb.
12|2|A song of praise: God is my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid.
13|11|A prophecy against Babylon: the day of the LORD is coming.
14|12|The proud king of Babylon falls like the morning star; God's purpose against Assyria stands.
15|5|A lament over the destruction of Moab.
16|5|Moab's fall; a throne will be set up in love in David's house.
17|10|A prophecy against Damascus and Israel, who forgot the God of their salvation.
18|7|A message to Cush: gifts will be brought to the LORD on Mount Zion.
19|25|Egypt will be struck, but will turn to the LORD; Egypt and Assyria will worship with Israel.
20|6|Isaiah walks barefoot as a sign that Egypt and Cush will be taken captive.
21|9|Babylon has fallen; prophecies against Edom and Arabia.
22|22|Jerusalem's leaders feast instead of repenting; the key of David is given to Eliakim.
23|9|A prophecy against Tyre, the proud trading city.
24|23|The LORD will judge the whole earth and reign on Mount Zion.
25|8|God will prepare a feast for all peoples and swallow up death for ever.
26|3|A song of trust: you keep in perfect peace the one whose mind is fixed on you.
27|13|God will care for his vineyard and gather his people.
28|16|Judgment on Ephraim's drunken leaders; God lays in Zion a precious cornerstone.
29|13|People honour God with their lips, but their hearts are far from him.
30|15|Do not trust Egypt; in returning and rest you shall be saved, in quietness and trust is your strength.
31|1|Woe to those who rely on Egypt's horses instead of the Holy One of Israel.
32|1|A king will reign in righteousness; the Spirit will be poured out.
33|22|The LORD is our judge, our lawgiver, our king; he will save us.
34|8|God's judgment on the nations and on Edom.
35|5|The desert will bloom; the blind will see and the lame will leap, as the redeemed return to Zion.
36|1|The Assyrian commander mocks Hezekiah and the LORD outside Jerusalem.
37|20|Hezekiah prays, and the angel of the LORD strikes down the Assyrian army.
38|17|Hezekiah is sick and prays; God adds fifteen years to his life.
39|6|Hezekiah shows his treasures to Babylon's envoys, and Isaiah foretells exile to Babylon.
40|31|Comfort my people. Prepare the way of the LORD; those who wait on the LORD will renew their strength.
41|10|Do not fear, for I am with you; God will help his servant Israel.
42|1|The LORD's chosen Servant will bring justice to the nations, gently.
43|2|When you pass through the waters I will be with you; I am doing a new thing.
44|6|I am the first and the last; besides me there is no god. The folly of idols. Cyrus will rebuild Jerusalem.
45|22|God anoints Cyrus; turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth.
46|4|Babylon's idols must be carried, but God carries his people to old age.
47|1|Proud Babylon will be brought down.
48|17|God refined Israel in affliction; I am the LORD who teaches you; go out from Babylon.
49|6|The Servant will be a light to the nations; God will never forget Zion.
50|7|The Servant obeys though he is beaten and spat upon, trusting God to help him.
51|11|Look to Abraham; the redeemed of the LORD will return with singing.
52|7|How beautiful the feet of those who bring good news. The Servant will be lifted up, though disfigured.
53|5|The suffering Servant: he was pierced for our transgressions, and by his wounds we are healed.
54|10|Sing, barren one; God's love and covenant of peace will not be removed.
55|1|Come, everyone who thirsts; seek the LORD while he may be found. My thoughts are not your thoughts.
56|7|Foreigners who hold to God's covenant are welcome; my house will be a house of prayer for all peoples.
57|15|God dwells with the humble and contrite; there is no peace for the wicked.
58|6|True fasting is to set the oppressed free and share bread with the hungry.
59|2|Your sins have separated you from God; the Redeemer will come to Zion.
60|1|Arise, shine, for your light has come; nations will come to Zion's light.
61|1|The Spirit of the LORD is on me to bring good news to the poor, words Jesus read in Nazareth.
62|5|God will not rest until Zion shines; he will rejoice over her as a bridegroom.
63|9|The LORD comes in judgment; a prayer remembering his mercy in the past.
64|8|A prayer: you are our Father, we are the clay and you the potter.
65|17|God answers: I am creating new heavens and a new earth.
66|2|God looks to the humble; all flesh will worship before him in the new creation.
