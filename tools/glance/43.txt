# John
fits 1-12: The life of Jesus: signs that show who he is, about AD 27–30
fits 13-21: The life of Jesus: the last night, his death and resurrection, about AD 30
1|14|In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was God; the Word became flesh. John the Baptist points to the Lamb of God, and the first disciples follow Jesus.
2|11|Jesus turns water into wine at a wedding in Cana, his first sign, and clears the temple.
3|16|Jesus tells Nicodemus he must be born again: God so loved the world that he gave his only Son.
4|14|Jesus offers living water to a Samaritan woman, and many believe; he heals an official's son.
5|24|Jesus heals a man at the pool on the Sabbath and says he does the Father's work.
6|35|Jesus feeds five thousand, walks on water, and says, "I am the bread of life." Many leave him.
7|37|At the Feast of Tabernacles Jesus invites the thirsty to come to him; people are divided.
8|12|A woman caught in adultery is forgiven. "I am the light of the world"; before Abraham was, I am.
9|25|Jesus heals a man born blind, who is questioned and believes.
10|11|"I am the good shepherd," who lays down his life for the sheep; I and the Father are one.
11|25|Jesus raises Lazarus from the dead: "I am the resurrection and the life." The leaders plot to kill him.
12|24|Mary anoints Jesus; he enters Jerusalem; a grain of wheat must die to bear fruit.
13|34|Jesus washes his disciples' feet and gives a new command: love one another.
14|6|"I am the way, the truth and the life." Jesus promises the Holy Spirit and his peace.
15|5|"I am the vine; you are the branches." Abide in me and love one another.
16|33|The Spirit will guide into truth; your sorrow will turn to joy; I have overcome the world.
17|3|Jesus prays for himself, his disciples and all who will believe.
18|37|Jesus is arrested, Peter denies him, and Jesus stands before Pilate.
19|30|Jesus is crucified; "It is finished." He is buried.
20|31|Mary Magdalene sees the risen Jesus; Thomas believes. These are written that you may believe.
21|17|Jesus appears by the sea, restores Peter and tells him to feed his sheep.
