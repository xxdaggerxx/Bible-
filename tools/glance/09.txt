# 1 Samuel
fits 1-7: Samuel, the last judge, about 1100–1050 BC
fits 8-15: Saul, Israel's first king, about 1050 BC
fits 16-31: David rises while Saul falls, about 1025–1010 BC
1|27|Hannah, who has no children, prays for a son at Shiloh. God answers, and she gives Samuel to the LORD.
2|2|Hannah's prayer of praise. Eli's sons are wicked priests, and God warns Eli of judgment.
3|10|The LORD calls the boy Samuel at night, and Samuel answers, "Speak, for your servant hears."
4|21|The Philistines defeat Israel and capture the ark. Eli's sons die, and Eli dies when he hears the news.
5|4|The ark brings disaster on the Philistines, and their idol Dagon falls before it.
6|20|The Philistines send the ark back on a cart.
7|12|Samuel calls Israel to turn back to God, and God defeats the Philistines. Samuel sets up a stone called Ebenezer.
8|7|The people ask for a king like the other nations. God says they have rejected him, but lets them have one.
9|17|Saul goes looking for lost donkeys and meets Samuel, who knows God has chosen him.
10|1|Samuel anoints Saul, who prophesies, and Saul is chosen king by lot.
11|15|Saul rescues the town of Jabesh-gilead from the Ammonites and is confirmed as king.
12|24|Samuel's farewell: he urges the people and their king to fear and serve the LORD.
13|14|Saul offers a sacrifice himself instead of waiting for Samuel. Samuel says God will seek a man after his own heart.
14|6|Jonathan and his armour-bearer attack a Philistine outpost, trusting God, and Israel wins.
15|22|Saul disobeys God's command about the Amalekites. To obey is better than sacrifice, and God rejects Saul as king.
16|7|Samuel anoints the young shepherd David, for the LORD looks at the heart. David plays music for troubled Saul.
17|47|David kills the giant Goliath with a sling and a stone, trusting the LORD.
18|1|Jonathan loves David as himself. Saul grows jealous of David's success and tries to kill him.
19|2|Saul tries to kill David again; Jonathan and Michal help him escape.
20|42|Jonathan warns David that Saul means to kill him, and they part as close friends.
21|6|David flees to Nob, where the priest gives him the holy bread and Goliath's sword.
22|23|David gathers men in a cave. Saul kills the priests of Nob for helping David.
23|16|David saves the town of Keilah and escapes Saul again; Jonathan strengthens his faith.
24|6|David spares Saul's life in a cave, refusing to harm the LORD's anointed.
25|32|Abigail stops David from taking revenge on her foolish husband Nabal, and later marries David.
26|23|David spares Saul again, taking only his spear and water jar.
27|1|David goes to live among the Philistines to escape Saul.
28|6|Saul, abandoned by God, visits a medium at Endor and hears that he will die.
29|9|The Philistine commanders send David away from the battle against Israel.
30|6|Raiders burn Ziklag and take the families; David strengthens himself in the LORD and recovers everything.
31|4|The Philistines defeat Israel on Mount Gilboa, and Saul and his sons die.
