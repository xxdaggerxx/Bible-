# Job
fits 1-42: Wisdom: the story of Job, set in the time of the patriarchs
1|21|Job is a good man whom God blesses. Satan says Job serves God only for gain, and Job loses his children and wealth, yet he worships God.
2|10|Satan strikes Job with painful sores. His wife tells him to curse God, but he will not. Three friends come to comfort him.
3|11|Job breaks his silence and curses the day he was born.
4|7|Eliphaz says the innocent never perish, so Job must have sinned.
5|17|Eliphaz urges Job to seek God and accept his discipline.
6|10|Job says his friends have failed him, and holds on to his innocence.
7|17|Job asks God why he is so troubled and pleads for relief.
8|20|Bildad says God never rejects the blameless, so Job's children must have sinned.
9|33|Job asks how a man can be right before God, and wishes for someone to stand between them.
10|8|Job asks God why he made him only to destroy him.
11|7|Zophar says Job deserves even worse and should repent.
12|10|Job answers that he knows God's power as well as they do.
13|15|Job will argue his case with God: though he slay me, I will hope in him.
14|14|Job reflects on how short life is: if a man dies, will he live again?
15|14|Eliphaz accuses Job of empty talk and of undermining reverence for God.
16|19|Job calls his friends miserable comforters; his witness is in heaven.
17|1|Job's spirit is broken and his hope seems gone.
18|5|Bildad describes the terrible end of the wicked.
19|25|Job feels abandoned by all, yet declares: I know that my Redeemer lives.
20|5|Zophar says the joy of the wicked is short.
21|7|Job answers that the wicked often prosper.
22|21|Eliphaz accuses Job of great wickedness and urges him to make peace with God.
23|10|Job longs to find God; yet he knows that God tests him and he will come out as gold.
24|1|Job asks why God seems to let the wicked oppress the poor.
25|4|Bildad says no man can be pure before God.
26|14|Job answers with a hymn to God's great power over creation.
27|6|Job holds fast to his integrity.
28|28|Where can wisdom be found? The fear of the Lord is wisdom.
29|2|Job remembers his past days of honour and blessing.
30|20|Job laments his present suffering and shame.
31|6|Job swears he has lived an upright life and calls on God to answer.
32|8|A younger man, Elihu, speaks up, angry at Job and at the friends.
33|14|Elihu says God speaks to people in dreams and through pain, to turn them back.
34|12|Elihu defends God's justice: God will never do wrong.
35|10|Elihu says people cry out in trouble but do not seek God.
36|15|Elihu says God uses suffering to teach and deliver.
37|23|Elihu speaks of God's majesty in the storm.
38|4|The LORD answers Job out of the whirlwind: where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth?
39|26|God asks Job about the wild animals and birds he cares for.
40|4|Job admits he is small and will say no more. God speaks of the great beast Behemoth.
41|11|God describes the mighty Leviathan: everything under heaven is his.
42|5|Job repents before God. God rebukes the friends and restores Job twice as much as before.
