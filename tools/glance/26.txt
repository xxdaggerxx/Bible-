# Ezekiel
fits 1-24: Ezekiel among the exiles in Babylon warns that Jerusalem will fall, 593–588 BC
fits 25-32: Prophecies against the nations
fits 33-48: After Jerusalem's fall: hope, restoration and the new temple, 586–573 BC
1|28|By the river Kebar in Babylon, Ezekiel sees a vision of God's glory on a throne above living creatures and wheels.
2|7|God sends Ezekiel to rebellious Israel, whether they listen or not.
3|17|Ezekiel eats the scroll and is made a watchman for Israel.
4|3|Ezekiel acts out the siege of Jerusalem with a clay brick.
5|11|Ezekiel shaves his head as a sign of Jerusalem's judgment.
6|7|Judgment on the mountains and idols of Israel.
7|2|The end has come upon the land.
8|6|In a vision Ezekiel sees idol worship in the temple itself.
9|4|Those who grieve over the sins are marked, and the rest are judged.
10|18|The glory of the LORD leaves the temple.
11|19|God will give his scattered people one heart and a new spirit. The glory leaves the city.
12|3|Ezekiel packs his bags as a sign of exile.
13|10|Against false prophets who say "Peace" when there is no peace.
14|14|Even Noah, Daniel and Job could only save themselves.
15|6|Jerusalem is like a useless vine, fit only for burning.
16|60|Jerusalem pictured as an unfaithful wife, yet God will remember his covenant.
17|24|A parable of two eagles and a vine; God will plant a tender shoot.
18|32|Each person is responsible for their own sin; God takes no pleasure in death: turn and live.
19|1|A lament for the princes of Israel.
20|44|Israel's history of rebellion and God's mercy for his name's sake.
21|27|The sword of the LORD against Jerusalem; it will be ruin until he comes whose right it is.
22|30|Jerusalem's sins; God looked for someone to stand in the gap and found none.
23|35|Two unfaithful sisters picture Samaria and Jerusalem.
24|18|The siege begins; Ezekiel's wife dies and he is told not to mourn, as a sign.
25|7|Prophecies against Ammon, Moab, Edom and Philistia.
26|3|A prophecy against Tyre, which rejoiced at Jerusalem's fall: it will become a bare rock.
27|3|A lament for Tyre, the proud trading ship.
28|2|Against the proud king of Tyre, and Sidon; Israel will be gathered.
29|3|A prophecy against Egypt and Pharaoh.
30|3|The day of the LORD for Egypt.
31|10|Egypt is like a great cedar that will be cut down.
32|2|A lament for Pharaoh and Egypt going down to the grave.
33|11|Ezekiel the watchman; God takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked. News comes that Jerusalem has fallen.
34|11|God will search for his scattered sheep and set one shepherd, David, over them.
35|15|A prophecy against Mount Seir (Edom).
36|26|God will give his people a new heart and a new spirit, for his name's sake.
37|5|The valley of dry bones comes to life by God's Spirit; Israel and Judah will be one.
38|23|Gog's great army attacks Israel and is defeated.
39|29|God's victory over Gog; he will pour out his Spirit on Israel.
40|4|A vision of a new temple, measured by a man of bronze.
41|1|The measurements of the temple building.
42|20|The priests' rooms and the outer measurements.
43|5|The glory of the LORD returns and fills the temple.
44|23|Rules for the priests in the new temple.
45|9|The holy district and fair rulers.
46|9|Worship and offerings in the new temple.
47|9|A river flows from the temple, bringing life wherever it goes.
48|35|The land divided among the tribes; the city's name is "The LORD is there."
