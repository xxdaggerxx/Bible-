# 1 John
fits 1-5: John writes to churches he cares for, about AD 85–95
1|9|We proclaim the Word of life; God is light. If we confess our sins, he is faithful to forgive us.
2|1|Jesus is our advocate; whoever says he knows God must keep his commands; do not love the world.
3|1|See what love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God; love one another in deed.
4|8|Test the spirits; God is love, and perfect love casts out fear.
5|13|Faith overcomes the world; whoever has the Son has life, that you may know you have eternal life.
