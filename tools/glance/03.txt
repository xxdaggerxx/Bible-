# Leviticus
fits 1-27: At Mount Sinai: laws for worship and holy living, about 1445 BC
1|4|How to bring a burnt offering: an animal without defect, wholly burned, given for acceptance before God.
2|1|How to bring a grain offering of flour, oil and incense, a gift of thanks to the LORD.
3|1|How to bring a peace offering, a shared meal of fellowship with God.
4|35|The sin offering for those who sin without meaning to: priests, the whole people, leaders and ordinary people.
5|5|The guilt offering: confessing sin and making things right, with a gift for those who are poor.
6|13|More instructions for the priests about the offerings and the fire on the altar that must never go out.
7|37|The priests' share of the offerings and the rules for the peace offerings.
8|12|Moses ordains Aaron and his sons as priests with washing, garments, anointing and sacrifice.
9|24|Aaron offers the first sacrifices, and fire comes from the LORD and consumes the offering. The people shout and fall on their faces.
10|3|Aaron's sons Nadab and Abihu offer unauthorised fire and die. God must be treated as holy.
11|44|Which animals are clean and may be eaten, and which are unclean. "Be holy, for I am holy."
12|8|How a mother is made clean again after giving birth.
13|46|How the priest is to examine skin diseases and mould on clothing.
14|20|How a person healed of a skin disease is cleansed, and what to do about mould in a house.
15|31|Uncleanness from bodily discharges, and how to be made clean.
16|30|The Day of Atonement: the high priest enters the Most Holy Place with blood, and the scapegoat carries the people's sins away.
17|11|Sacrifices must be brought to the tabernacle, and blood must not be eaten, for the life is in the blood.
18|5|God forbids the sexual sins of Egypt and Canaan; his people are to live by his ways.
19|18|Laws for holy living in everyday life: honour parents, care for the poor and the foreigner, and love your neighbour as yourself.
20|26|Punishments for idolatry, for sins against family and for sexual sins. Israel is set apart to be God's own.
21|8|Rules of holiness for the priests and the high priest.
22|32|How priests must handle the holy offerings, and offerings must be without defect.
23|2|The yearly feasts: Sabbath, Passover, Firstfruits, Weeks, Trumpets, the Day of Atonement and Tabernacles.
24|2|The lamp and the holy bread. A man who blasphemes God's name is put to death.
25|10|The Sabbath year and the Year of Jubilee, when land returns to its families and slaves are freed.
26|12|Blessings if Israel obeys and curses if they rebel, with God's promise to remember his covenant when they repent.
27|30|Rules for vows and for things given to the LORD, including the tithe.
