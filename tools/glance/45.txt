# Romans
fits 1-16: Paul's letter to the church in Rome, about AD 57
1|16|Paul is not ashamed of the gospel, the power of God for salvation. People have rejected God and turned to idols and sin.
2|11|God judges everyone fairly; having the law does not save those who break it.
3|23|All have sinned and fall short of God's glory, and are justified freely by his grace through faith in Christ.
4|3|Abraham was counted righteous by faith, not by works.
5|8|At peace with God through faith; God showed his love in that Christ died for us while we were sinners. Adam and Christ.
6|23|Dead to sin and alive to God; the wages of sin is death, but God's gift is eternal life.
7|24|The law shows sin; the struggle with sin within: who will rescue me?
8|28|No condemnation for those in Christ. Life in the Spirit; all things work together for good; nothing can separate us from God's love.
9|16|Paul grieves for Israel; God's mercy and choice.
10|9|If you confess Jesus as Lord and believe God raised him, you will be saved; faith comes by hearing.
11|33|God has not rejected Israel; Gentiles are grafted in. Oh the depth of the riches of God's wisdom!
12|2|Offer yourselves as living sacrifices; be transformed by renewing your mind; love sincerely.
13|10|Respect authorities; love fulfils the law; put on the Lord Jesus Christ.
14|19|Do not judge one another over food and days; build each other up.
15|13|Accept one another as Christ accepted you. Paul's plans to visit Rome.
16|20|Paul greets many friends in Rome and warns against those who cause divisions.
