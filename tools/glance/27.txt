# Daniel
fits 1-6: Daniel in Babylon and Persia, about 605–536 BC
fits 7-12: Daniel's visions of the kingdoms to come
1|8|Daniel and his friends are taken to Babylon; they refuse the king's food and God gives them wisdom.
2|44|Daniel tells Nebuchadnezzar his dream of a great statue: God's kingdom will crush all others and last for ever.
3|17|Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego refuse to bow to the gold image and are thrown into the furnace, but God saves them.
4|34|Nebuchadnezzar becomes like an animal until he learns that the Most High rules, and then praises God.
5|27|At Belshazzar's feast a hand writes on the wall; Daniel reads it, and that night Babylon falls.
6|22|Daniel keeps praying and is thrown to the lions, but God shuts their mouths.
7|13|Daniel's vision of four beasts and one like a son of man given everlasting dominion.
8|19|A vision of a ram and a goat: the kingdoms of Persia and Greece.
9|19|Daniel confesses Israel's sin and prays; Gabriel brings the prophecy of seventy weeks.
10|19|A glorious messenger strengthens Daniel and speaks of spiritual battles.
11|32|Kings of the north and south; those who know their God will stand firm.
12|2|At the end, many who sleep will awake; the wise will shine like the stars.
