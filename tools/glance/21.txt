# Ecclesiastes
fits 1-12: Wisdom: the Preacher (traditionally Solomon) on the meaning of life, about 935 BC
1|2|The Preacher says everything under the sun is vanity, a fleeting breath; nothing is new.
2|24|He tried pleasure, great projects and wealth, and found them empty without God.
3|1|There is a time for everything; God has set eternity in our hearts.
4|9|Oppression, envy and loneliness; two are better than one.
5|2|Be careful when you come before God, and do not love money.
6|12|Riches cannot satisfy; who knows what is good for a person?
7|14|Wise sayings about sorrow, patience and the limits of wisdom.
8|12|Obey the king; it will go well with those who fear God, though life is hard to understand.
9|10|Everyone dies; so enjoy life with those you love and work with all your might.
10|1|Wisdom and folly in leaders and in everyday work.
11|1|Cast your bread on the waters; enjoy your youth, but remember God will judge.
12|13|Remember your Creator in your youth. The end of the matter: fear God and keep his commandments.
