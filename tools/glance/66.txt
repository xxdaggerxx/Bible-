# Revelation
fits 1-3: John's vision on Patmos and the letters to seven churches, about AD 95
fits 4-22: John's visions of heaven, judgment and the new creation
1|8|John on Patmos sees the risen Jesus among seven lampstands: I am the Alpha and the Omega.
2|10|Letters to the churches in Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum and Thyatira: hold fast and overcome.
3|20|Letters to Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea: I stand at the door and knock.
4|8|A door opens in heaven: God on his throne, worshipped day and night: holy, holy, holy.
5|9|Only the Lamb who was slain is worthy to open the scroll, and all creation praises him.
6|17|The Lamb opens six seals: conquest, war, famine, death, martyrs crying out, and a great earthquake.
7|17|God's servants are sealed; a great multitude from every nation stands before the throne.
8|1|The seventh seal; the first four trumpets bring judgment on the earth.
9|20|The fifth and sixth trumpets bring terrible plagues, yet people do not repent.
10|7|A mighty angel with a little scroll; John eats it and must prophesy again.
11|15|Two witnesses prophesy, are killed and rise; the seventh trumpet: the kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord.
12|11|A woman, her child and the dragon; war in heaven; they overcame by the blood of the Lamb.
13|8|A beast from the sea and a beast from the earth demand worship; the mark of the beast.
14|13|The Lamb on Mount Zion; angels announce the gospel and judgment; the harvest of the earth.
15|3|The song of Moses and the Lamb; seven angels with seven last plagues.
16|1|The seven bowls of God's wrath are poured out.
17|14|The great prostitute Babylon riding the beast; the Lamb will conquer, for he is Lord of lords.
18|2|Babylon the great has fallen, and the world mourns her.
19|6|Heaven rejoices; the marriage supper of the Lamb; Christ rides out as King of kings and defeats the beast.
20|12|Satan is bound for a thousand years, then defeated for ever; the dead are judged before the great white throne.
21|4|A new heaven and a new earth; God will dwell with his people and wipe away every tear. The new Jerusalem.
22|20|The river of life and the tree of life; Jesus says, "I am coming soon." Come, Lord Jesus.
