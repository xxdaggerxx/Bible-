# Jeremiah
fits 1-25: Jeremiah warns Judah before Babylon comes, about 627–605 BC
fits 26-45: Jeremiah's sufferings and the fall of Jerusalem, about 605–586 BC
fits 46-51: Prophecies against the nations
fits 52-52: The fall of Jerusalem, 586 BC
1|5|God calls young Jeremiah: before I formed you in the womb I knew you. He will be a prophet to the nations.
2|13|Israel has forsaken God, the fountain of living water, for broken cisterns.
3|22|Return, faithless people, and I will heal your faithlessness.
4|3|Judah must turn back or disaster will come from the north.
5|1|Search Jerusalem for one honest person; the people have refused to repent.
6|16|Stand at the crossroads and ask for the ancient paths, but the people refuse.
7|4|Jeremiah's temple sermon: do not trust in the temple while living in sin.
8|22|The people do not know God's ways; is there no balm in Gilead?
9|24|Jeremiah weeps for his people. Let the one who boasts boast in knowing the LORD.
10|10|Idols are worthless; the LORD is the true and living God.
11|11|Judah has broken the covenant; plots against Jeremiah's life.
12|1|Jeremiah asks why the wicked prosper; God says worse is coming.
13|11|A ruined linen belt pictures how God will ruin Judah's pride.
14|7|Drought; Jeremiah prays for the people, and God warns against false prophets.
15|16|Jeremiah complains of his suffering; God's words were his joy, and God promises to make him strong.
16|2|Jeremiah is told not to marry, as a sign of coming judgment.
17|7|Blessed is the one who trusts in the LORD; the heart is deceitful above all things.
18|6|At the potter's house: as clay in the potter's hand, so is Israel in God's hand.
19|11|Jeremiah breaks a clay jar as a sign that Jerusalem will be broken.
20|9|Jeremiah is beaten and put in stocks; God's word is like fire shut up in his bones.
21|8|King Zedekiah asks for help; Jerusalem will fall to Babylon.
22|16|Warnings to Judah's kings to do justice.
23|5|Woe to the shepherds who scatter the flock; God will raise up a righteous Branch for David.
24|7|Two baskets of figs: God will care for the exiles in Babylon and give them a heart to know him.
25|11|Judah will serve Babylon seventy years; the cup of God's wrath for the nations.
26|13|Jeremiah is put on trial for prophesying against the temple, but is spared.
27|12|Jeremiah wears a yoke: serve the king of Babylon and live.
28|9|The false prophet Hananiah breaks the yoke and promises a quick return, and dies.
29|11|Jeremiah's letter to the exiles: settle in Babylon and pray for it; I know the plans I have for you.
30|17|God will restore the fortunes of Israel and Judah.
31|31|God will make a new covenant, writing his law on their hearts.
32|27|Jeremiah buys a field during the siege as a sign that land will be bought again.
33|3|Call to me and I will answer you; God will restore Judah and the line of David.
34|17|Zedekiah will be taken to Babylon; the people break their promise to free their slaves.
35|18|The Rechabites' faithfulness to their father shames Judah's unfaithfulness to God.
36|3|King Jehoiakim burns the scroll of Jeremiah's words, and it is written again.
37|17|Jeremiah is arrested and imprisoned as a deserter.
38|6|Jeremiah is thrown into a muddy cistern and rescued by Ebed-melech.
39|18|Jerusalem falls to Babylon. Jeremiah is freed and Ebed-melech is spared.
40|4|Jeremiah stays in Judah with Gedaliah, the new governor.
41|2|Ishmael murders the governor Gedaliah, and the people fear what Babylon will do.
42|11|The people ask Jeremiah to pray; God tells them to stay in Judah and not go to Egypt.
43|7|The people disobey and take Jeremiah to Egypt.
44|23|Jeremiah warns the Jews in Egypt against worshipping the queen of heaven.
45|5|A message for Baruch, Jeremiah's scribe: do not seek great things for yourself.
46|28|A prophecy against Egypt, and comfort for Israel.
47|4|A prophecy against the Philistines.
48|47|A prophecy against Moab, with hope of restoration.
49|6|Prophecies against Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar and Elam.
50|34|Babylon will fall; their Redeemer is strong.
51|15|More about Babylon's fall; God made the earth by his power.
52|12|Jerusalem is destroyed and the people taken to Babylon; later King Jehoiachin is released from prison.
