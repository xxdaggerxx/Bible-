# Matthew
fits 1-4: The life of Jesus: birth and beginnings, about 5 BC – AD 27
fits 5-20: The life of Jesus: teaching and miracles in Galilee and on the way to Jerusalem, about AD 27–30
fits 21-28: The life of Jesus: the last week in Jerusalem, his death and resurrection, about AD 30
1|21|The family line of Jesus from Abraham through David. An angel tells Joseph that Mary's child is from the Holy Spirit: call him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.
2|11|Wise men from the east follow a star to worship the child. Herod tries to kill him, and Joseph takes the family to Egypt, then to Nazareth.
3|17|John the Baptist calls people to repent. Jesus is baptized, the Spirit comes on him, and the Father says, "This is my beloved Son."
4|19|Jesus is tempted in the wilderness and answers with Scripture. He begins preaching in Galilee and calls his first disciples.
5|3|The Sermon on the Mount begins: the Beatitudes, salt and light, and Jesus' teaching on anger, lust, honesty and loving enemies.
6|33|Jesus teaches about giving, prayer (the Lord's Prayer) and fasting in secret, and not worrying: seek first God's kingdom.
7|12|Do not judge; ask, seek, knock; the golden rule; the narrow gate; build your house on the rock.
8|27|Jesus heals a leper, a centurion's servant and many others, and calms a storm.
9|13|Jesus forgives and heals a paralysed man, calls Matthew, raises a girl and heals the blind.
10|39|Jesus sends out the twelve apostles and warns them of persecution.
11|28|Jesus answers John's doubts and invites the weary: come to me and I will give you rest.
12|8|Jesus is Lord of the Sabbath; the Pharisees plot against him and accuse him of working by Satan.
13|23|Parables of the kingdom: the sower, the weeds, the mustard seed, the hidden treasure and the net.
14|27|John the Baptist is killed. Jesus feeds five thousand and walks on water.
15|28|Jesus challenges empty tradition, heals a Canaanite woman's daughter and feeds four thousand.
16|16|Peter confesses, "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God." Jesus foretells his death.
17|5|Jesus is transfigured on the mountain; he heals a boy and pays the temple tax.
18|21|Greatness in the kingdom, the lost sheep, and forgiving seventy times seven.
19|26|Jesus teaches about marriage, blesses children, and meets a rich young man.
20|28|The workers in the vineyard. The Son of Man came to serve and give his life as a ransom.
21|9|Jesus enters Jerusalem on a donkey, clears the temple and tells parables against the leaders.
22|37|The wedding feast; questions about taxes and the resurrection; the greatest commandment.
23|37|Jesus pronounces woes on the hypocrisy of the scribes and Pharisees and weeps over Jerusalem.
24|35|Jesus foretells the temple's fall and his coming again; keep watch.
25|40|Parables of the ten virgins and the talents; the judgment of the sheep and the goats.
26|28|The plot to kill Jesus, the Last Supper, his prayer in Gethsemane, his arrest and Peter's denial.
27|54|Jesus is tried before Pilate, crucified and buried.
28|19|Jesus rises from the dead and sends his disciples to make disciples of all nations.
