# Lamentations
fits 1-5: Laments after Jerusalem fell to Babylon, 586 BC
1|1|Jerusalem sits lonely like a widow, weeping, with no one to comfort her.
2|17|The LORD has done what he planned; Jerusalem's walls and temple lie in ruins.
3|23|In the depths of grief, hope: the LORD's mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning.
4|22|The horrors of the siege; Zion's punishment will end.
5|21|A prayer: remember us, LORD; restore us to yourself.
