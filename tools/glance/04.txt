# Numbers
fits 1-10: At Mount Sinai, about 1445 BC
fits 11-20: In the wilderness: forty years of wandering, about 1445–1407 BC
fits 21-36: On the plains of Moab, about 1407 BC
1|3|Moses counts the fighting men of each tribe. The Levites are set apart to care for the tabernacle.
2|34|How the tribes camp around the tabernacle and march in order.
3|12|The Levites are counted and given to the LORD in place of Israel's firstborn.
4|49|The duties of the Levite clans in carrying the tabernacle.
5|7|Unclean people are kept outside the camp; wrongs must be confessed and repaid; the test for a wife suspected of unfaithfulness.
6|24|The Nazirite vow, and the priestly blessing: "The LORD bless you and keep you."
7|89|The leaders of the tribes bring gifts to dedicate the altar.
8|14|The lampstand is set up, and the Levites are cleansed and set apart.
9|17|The second Passover. The cloud over the tabernacle shows Israel when to move and when to stay.
10|35|Silver trumpets are made. Israel leaves Sinai, following the ark.
11|23|The people complain about the manna and crave meat. God gives seventy elders his Spirit, and sends quail with a plague.
12|7|Miriam and Aaron speak against Moses, and God defends Moses, his faithful servant. Miriam is struck with a skin disease.
13|30|Twelve spies explore Canaan. Ten say the people are too strong, but Caleb says they can take it.
14|24|The people refuse to enter Canaan. God says that generation will die in the wilderness; only Caleb and Joshua will enter.
15|41|More laws for offerings, and tassels on clothing to remind Israel of God's commands.
16|40|Korah, Dathan and Abiram rebel against Moses and Aaron, and the ground swallows them.
17|8|Aaron's staff buds, showing God has chosen him as priest.
18|20|The duties and share of the priests and Levites.
19|9|The ashes of a red heifer are used to cleanse those made unclean by a dead body.
20|12|Miriam dies. Moses strikes the rock in anger instead of speaking to it, and may not enter Canaan. Aaron dies.
21|9|Israel complains, and God sends snakes; those who look at the bronze snake live. Israel defeats Sihon and Og.
22|31|King Balak of Moab hires Balaam to curse Israel. Balaam's donkey sees the angel of the LORD.
23|19|Balaam tries to curse Israel but can only bless them, for God is not a man that he should lie.
24|17|Balaam blesses Israel again and foretells a star rising from Jacob.
25|11|Israel joins in Moab's idol worship and sexual sin. Phinehas stops the plague with his zeal.
26|65|A second census of the new generation before entering Canaan.
27|18|The daughters of Zelophehad receive their father's land. Joshua is chosen to lead after Moses.
28|2|The daily, Sabbath, monthly and Passover offerings.
29|1|The offerings for the autumn feasts.
30|2|Rules for vows made by men and by women.
31|49|Israel takes vengeance on Midian.
32|23|The tribes of Reuben and Gad, and half of Manasseh, settle east of the Jordan, promising to help conquer Canaan.
33|53|A list of Israel's journeys from Egypt to the plains of Moab.
34|2|The borders of the promised land and the leaders who will divide it.
35|11|Towns for the Levites and cities of refuge for those who kill someone by accident.
36|7|Women who inherit land must marry within their tribe, so each tribe keeps its land.
