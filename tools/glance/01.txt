# Genesis
fits 1-11: Beginnings: creation, the fall and the flood, before Abraham
fits 12-25: The patriarchs: Abraham, about 2000 BC
fits 26-36: The patriarchs: Isaac and Jacob, about 1900 BC
fits 37-50: The patriarchs: Joseph in Egypt, about 1900–1800 BC
1|27|God creates the heavens and the earth in six days, by his word, and everything he makes is good. On the sixth day he makes man and woman in his own image to care for the world.
2|7|God rests on the seventh day. A closer look at the making of Adam from the dust, the garden of Eden, and the first marriage, when God makes Eve and brings her to Adam.
3|15|The serpent tempts Eve, and she and Adam eat the forbidden fruit. Sin brings shame, pain and death, but God promises that a son of the woman will crush the serpent's head.
4|7|Cain kills his brother Abel out of jealousy and is sent away. Sin spreads through Cain's family, while Adam and Eve have another son, Seth, and people begin to call on the LORD.
5|24|The family line from Adam to Noah, with their long lives and the refrain "and he died". Enoch is different: he walked with God, and God took him.
6|8|The earth is full of violence and God grieves that he made mankind. But Noah finds favour, and God tells him to build an ark to save his family and the animals.
7|23|Noah, his family and the animals go into the ark, and God shuts them in. The rain falls for forty days, and the flood covers the earth.
8|22|The waters go down and the ark rests on the mountains of Ararat. Noah sends out birds, leaves the ark and offers a sacrifice, and God promises never again to curse the ground.
9|13|God blesses Noah and makes a covenant never to destroy the earth by flood, with the rainbow as its sign. Noah gets drunk, and Ham dishonours him.
10|32|The nations that came from Noah's sons Shem, Ham and Japheth, spreading over the earth after the flood.
11|9|People build a tower at Babel to make a name for themselves, and God confuses their language and scatters them. The line of Shem leads to Abram.
12|3|God calls Abram to leave his homeland and promises to make him a great nation and to bless all nations through him. Abram goes to Canaan, then to Egypt in a famine.
13|15|Abram and his nephew Lot part ways because their flocks are too large. Lot chooses the land near Sodom, and God promises all the land to Abram's children.
14|20|Abram rescues Lot after raiding kings carry him off. The priest-king Melchizedek blesses Abram, and Abram gives him a tenth.
15|6|God promises Abram a son and children as many as the stars, and Abram believes him; it is counted to him as righteousness. God seals the promise with a covenant.
16|13|Sarai gives her servant Hagar to Abram, and Hagar conceives. She runs away, and the LORD finds her in the desert and promises a son, Ishmael.
17|7|God makes his covenant with Abram again, renames him Abraham and Sarai Sarah, and gives circumcision as its sign. He promises Sarah will bear a son, Isaac.
18|14|Three visitors come to Abraham, and the LORD repeats that Sarah will have a son; Sarah laughs. Abraham pleads with God for the city of Sodom.
19|29|Two angels rescue Lot and his daughters before God destroys Sodom and Gomorrah. Lot's wife looks back and becomes a pillar of salt.
20|6|Abraham again says Sarah is his sister, this time to King Abimelech of Gerar. God warns Abimelech in a dream and keeps Sarah safe.
21|1|Isaac is born, as God promised. Hagar and Ishmael are sent away, and God cares for them in the desert. Abraham makes a treaty with Abimelech at Beersheba.
22|8|God tests Abraham by asking him to offer Isaac. Abraham obeys, and God stops him and provides a ram in Isaac's place, renewing his promise to bless all nations.
23|19|Sarah dies, and Abraham buys the cave of Machpelah at Hebron to bury her, his first piece of the promised land.
24|27|Abraham's servant travels to find a wife for Isaac among his relatives. God guides him to Rebekah, who agrees to go and marry Isaac.
25|23|Abraham dies. Rebekah has twins, Esau and Jacob, and God says the older will serve the younger. Esau sells his birthright to Jacob for a meal.
26|24|God repeats his promises to Isaac. Isaac lives among the Philistines, digs wells, and makes peace with Abimelech.
27|28|Jacob, helped by his mother Rebekah, tricks his blind father Isaac into giving him Esau's blessing. Esau plans to kill him.
28|15|Jacob flees to his uncle Laban. At Bethel he dreams of a stairway to heaven, and God promises to be with him and bring him back.
29|20|Jacob works for Laban seven years to marry Rachel, but Laban tricks him into marrying Leah first. Leah has sons while Rachel has none.
30|22|Rachel and Leah compete for Jacob's love through their children and their servants. Rachel at last has Joseph, and Jacob's flocks grow rich.
31|3|God tells Jacob to go home. He leaves secretly with his family and flocks, Laban chases him, and they part with a covenant.
32|28|Jacob prepares to meet Esau in fear. At night he wrestles with God, who renames him Israel.
33|4|Esau runs to meet Jacob and embraces him, and the brothers are reconciled. Jacob settles near Shechem.
34|7|Shechem defiles Jacob's daughter Dinah. Her brothers Simeon and Levi take revenge by killing the men of the city.
35|11|God sends Jacob back to Bethel and confirms his new name and the promises. Rachel dies giving birth to Benjamin, and Isaac dies.
36|8|The family of Esau, who settled in Edom, and the chiefs and kings of the Edomites.
37|28|Jacob loves Joseph most and gives him a fine robe. Joseph dreams of ruling his brothers, and in jealousy they sell him as a slave to Egypt.
38|26|Judah wrongs his daughter-in-law Tamar, who tricks him to gain the children she is owed. Judah admits she was more righteous than he.
39|2|Joseph serves Potiphar faithfully and the LORD is with him. He refuses Potiphar's wife, who lies about him, and he is put in prison.
40|8|In prison Joseph explains the dreams of Pharaoh's cupbearer and baker. It happens as he said, but the cupbearer forgets him.
41|38|Joseph explains Pharaoh's dreams of seven good years and seven years of famine. Pharaoh puts him in charge of all Egypt to prepare.
42|21|Joseph's brothers come to Egypt to buy grain and bow before him without knowing him. He tests them and keeps Simeon until they bring Benjamin.
43|30|The brothers return with Benjamin. Joseph is moved to tears and holds a feast for them.
44|33|Joseph hides his cup in Benjamin's sack to test his brothers. Judah offers himself as a slave in Benjamin's place.
45|5|Joseph tells his brothers who he is and forgives them, seeing that God sent him ahead to save lives. He sends for his father.
46|3|God tells Jacob not to be afraid to go down to Egypt. Jacob and all his family travel there and he sees Joseph again.
47|27|Jacob's family settles in Goshen and Jacob blesses Pharaoh. Joseph manages Egypt through the famine.
48|16|Jacob blesses Joseph's sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, putting the younger first.
49|10|Jacob blesses his twelve sons before he dies, saying the sceptre will not depart from Judah. He asks to be buried in Canaan.
50|20|Joseph buries Jacob in Canaan and reassures his brothers: what they meant for evil, God meant for good. Joseph dies, sure that God will bring Israel home.
