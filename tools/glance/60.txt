# 1 Peter
fits 1-5: Peter writes to suffering Christians in Asia Minor, about AD 62–64
1|3|God has given us new birth into a living hope; be holy, for you were redeemed by Christ's precious blood.
2|9|You are a chosen people, a royal priesthood; Christ suffered for you, leaving an example.
3|15|Wives and husbands; repay evil with blessing; always be ready to give a reason for your hope.
4|10|Do not be surprised by suffering; use your gifts to serve one another.
5|7|Elders, shepherd the flock; cast all your anxiety on him, because he cares for you.
