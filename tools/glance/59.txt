# James
fits 1-5: James, the Lord's brother, writes to Jewish Christians, about AD 45–50
1|22|Count trials joy, ask God for wisdom, and be doers of the word, not hearers only.
2|17|Do not show favouritism; faith without works is dead.
3|17|The tongue is a fire hard to tame; wisdom from above is pure and peaceable.
4|8|Quarrels come from selfish desires; humble yourselves, draw near to God, and he will draw near to you.
5|16|Warning to the rich; be patient; pray for one another, for the prayer of a righteous person is powerful.
