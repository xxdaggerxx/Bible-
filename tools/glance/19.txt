# Psalms
fits 1-41: Psalms, Book 1: mostly songs of David, about 1000 BC
fits 42-72: Psalms, Book 2: songs of the sons of Korah, Asaph and David
fits 73-89: Psalms, Book 3: songs of Asaph and others, some from the time of the exile
fits 90-106: Psalms, Book 4: from Moses' prayer to songs of God's kingship
fits 107-150: Psalms, Book 5: songs of thanks, ascent and praise, many from after the exile
