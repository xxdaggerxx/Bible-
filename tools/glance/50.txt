# Philippians
fits 1-4: Paul's letter to the church in Philippi, written from prison, about AD 61
1|21|Paul thanks God for the Philippians; his chains have spread the gospel. For to me, to live is Christ and to die is gain.
2|5|Have the mind of Christ, who humbled himself to death on a cross and was exalted. Timothy and Epaphroditus.
3|14|Paul counts everything loss compared to knowing Christ, and presses on toward the goal.
4|13|Rejoice in the Lord; do not be anxious but pray; I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.
