# Zechariah
fits 1-8: Zechariah encourages the returned exiles to rebuild, 520–518 BC
fits 9-14: Prophecies of the coming King and the last days
1|3|Return to me and I will return to you. A vision of horsemen; God will comfort Zion.
2|10|A man measures Jerusalem; God will be a wall of fire around it and live among his people.
3|8|The high priest Joshua is cleansed of filthy clothes; God will bring his servant the Branch.
4|6|A lampstand and two olive trees: not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit.
5|3|A flying scroll and a woman in a basket: sin will be removed from the land.
6|12|Four chariots; the Branch will build the temple and be priest and king.
7|9|God wants justice and mercy, not just fasting.
8|8|God will return to Zion and bless it; many nations will seek the LORD.
9|9|Your king comes to you, righteous and humble, riding on a donkey.
10|6|God will strengthen Judah and gather his scattered people.
11|12|The shepherd rejected, paid thirty pieces of silver.
12|10|Jerusalem will look on the one they have pierced and mourn.
13|1|A fountain will be opened to cleanse sin; strike the shepherd and the sheep will scatter.
14|9|The day of the LORD: he will stand on the Mount of Olives and be king over all the earth.
