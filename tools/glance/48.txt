# Galatians
fits 1-6: Paul's letter to the churches of Galatia, about AD 48–49
1|6|Paul is astonished that the Galatians are turning to a different gospel; his gospel came from Christ.
2|20|Paul confronts Peter; we are justified by faith in Christ, not works of the law. I have been crucified with Christ.
3|11|Did you receive the Spirit by the law or by faith? The righteous shall live by faith; all are one in Christ.
4|7|You are no longer slaves but sons and heirs; Hagar and Sarah as two covenants.
5|1|Stand firm in freedom. Walk by the Spirit; the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace.
6|2|Bear one another's burdens; you reap what you sow; boast only in the cross.
