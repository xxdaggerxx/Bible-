# Ruth
fits 1-4: In the days of the judges, about 1100 BC
1|16|Naomi loses her husband and sons in Moab. Her daughter-in-law Ruth refuses to leave her: "Your people will be my people, and your God my God."
2|12|Ruth gleans in the fields of Boaz, a relative of Naomi, who treats her with great kindness.
3|11|Naomi sends Ruth to Boaz at the threshing floor to ask him to redeem her, and he promises to do it.
4|17|Boaz redeems Naomi's land and marries Ruth. Their son Obed is the grandfather of King David.
