# Ephesians
fits 1-6: Paul's letter to the church in Ephesus, written from prison, about AD 60–62
1|7|Every spiritual blessing in Christ: chosen, redeemed, forgiven and sealed with the Spirit. Paul prays for them.
2|8|Dead in sin, made alive by grace through faith; Jews and Gentiles are one new people in Christ.
3|20|The mystery revealed: Gentiles share in the promise. Paul prays that they may know Christ's love.
4|32|Live worthy of your calling in unity; the gifts Christ gives; put off the old self, put on the new.
5|1|Imitate God and walk in love and light; wives and husbands, as the church and Christ.
6|11|Children and parents, servants and masters; put on the whole armour of God.
