# Luke
fits 1-4: The life of Jesus: birth and beginnings, about 6 BC – AD 27
fits 5-9: The life of Jesus: ministry in Galilee, about AD 27–29
fits 10-19: The life of Jesus: the journey to Jerusalem, about AD 29–30
fits 20-24: The life of Jesus: the last week, his death and resurrection, about AD 30
1|37|Gabriel announces the births of John the Baptist and of Jesus. Mary visits Elizabeth and praises God; Zechariah prophesies.
2|11|Jesus is born in Bethlehem and shepherds hear the news. He is presented in the temple, and at twelve he stays in his Father's house.
3|22|John the Baptist preaches repentance and baptizes Jesus. The family line of Jesus back to Adam.
4|18|Jesus is tempted, then reads Isaiah in Nazareth: the Spirit is on me to preach good news to the poor. He heals many.
5|32|Jesus calls fishermen after a great catch, heals a leper and a paralysed man, and calls Levi.
6|31|Jesus chooses the twelve and teaches: blessed are the poor; love your enemies.
7|50|Jesus heals a centurion's servant, raises a widow's son, and forgives a sinful woman who anoints his feet.
8|21|The parable of the sower; Jesus calms a storm, frees a demon-possessed man, and raises Jairus's daughter.
9|23|Jesus sends the twelve, feeds five thousand, is confessed as the Christ, and is transfigured.
10|27|Jesus sends out seventy-two. The parable of the good Samaritan; Mary and Martha.
11|9|The Lord's Prayer; ask, seek, knock; woes on the Pharisees.
12|34|Beware of greed; do not worry; be ready for the Master's return.
13|24|Repent or perish; Jesus heals on the Sabbath; strive to enter the narrow door.
14|11|Humility at the feast; the great banquet; the cost of following Jesus.
15|7|Parables of the lost sheep, the lost coin and the lost son: joy in heaven over one sinner who repents.
16|13|The shrewd manager; you cannot serve God and money; the rich man and Lazarus.
17|21|Forgiveness and faith; ten lepers healed, one returns to thank him; the coming of the kingdom.
18|13|The persistent widow; the Pharisee and the tax collector; the rich ruler; a blind man healed.
19|10|Zacchaeus meets Jesus: the Son of Man came to seek and save the lost. Jesus enters Jerusalem and weeps over it.
20|17|Jesus' authority is questioned; the tenants; taxes and the resurrection.
21|33|The widow's offering; signs of the end and the Son of Man's coming.
22|19|The Last Supper, Jesus' prayer on the Mount of Olives, his arrest and Peter's denial.
23|34|Jesus before Pilate and Herod; crucified, he forgives and promises paradise to a thief; he is buried.
24|6|The empty tomb; Jesus walks with two disciples to Emmaus, appears to the eleven and ascends.
