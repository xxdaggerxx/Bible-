# Mark
fits 1-9: The life of Jesus: ministry in Galilee, about AD 27–29
fits 10-16: The life of Jesus: to Jerusalem, his death and resurrection, about AD 30
1|15|John baptizes Jesus. Jesus is tempted, calls fishermen, and heals many: the kingdom of God is at hand.
2|17|Jesus forgives and heals a paralysed man and calls Levi; he came to call sinners.
3|35|Jesus heals on the Sabbath, chooses the twelve, and is accused of being possessed.
4|41|Parables of the sower and the mustard seed; Jesus calms the storm.
5|19|Jesus frees a man from many demons, heals a bleeding woman and raises Jairus's daughter.
6|34|Jesus is rejected at home, sends out the twelve, feeds five thousand and walks on water.
7|15|What defiles a person comes from the heart; Jesus heals a Gentile girl and a deaf man.
8|29|Jesus feeds four thousand; Peter says, "You are the Christ"; take up your cross.
9|7|The transfiguration; Jesus heals a boy and teaches who is greatest.
10|45|Divorce, children, the rich man, and James and John's request; the Son of Man came to serve.
11|17|Jesus enters Jerusalem, curses a fig tree and clears the temple.
12|30|The tenants; questions on taxes and resurrection; the greatest commandment; the widow's gift.
13|31|Jesus foretells the temple's fall and his return; stay awake.
14|36|Jesus is anointed at Bethany, shares the Last Supper, prays in Gethsemane, is arrested and denied.
15|39|Jesus is tried before Pilate, mocked, crucified and buried.
16|6|The women find the tomb empty: he has risen. Jesus appears and sends out his disciples.
