# Deuteronomy
fits 1-34: Moses' last words on the plains of Moab, about 1406 BC
1|21|Moses reminds the new generation how their parents refused to enter the land from Kadesh.
2|7|Moses recalls the years of wandering and the victory over Sihon.
3|22|Moses recalls the defeat of Og and the land east of the Jordan. God lets him see the land but not enter it.
4|29|Moses urges Israel to obey God and not make idols, for there is no other god. If they seek him, they will find him.
5|6|Moses repeats the Ten Commandments given at Sinai.
6|5|"Hear, O Israel": love the LORD your God with all your heart, and teach his words to your children.
7|9|Israel must destroy the idols of Canaan, for God chose them out of his love, not because they were great.
8|3|God humbled Israel in the desert to teach that man does not live by bread alone. Do not forget him when you are rich.
9|6|Israel will win the land not because they are righteous; Moses recalls the golden calf.
10|12|God gives new tablets. What does the LORD require? To fear him, walk in his ways and love him.
11|18|The blessings of obedience; keep God's words on your hearts and teach them to your children.
12|5|Israel is to worship only at the place God chooses, not at the pagan high places.
13|4|How to deal with false prophets and anyone who leads others to idols.
14|2|Clean and unclean food, and the tithe.
15|11|Debts are cancelled every seventh year; be generous to the poor and free Hebrew slaves.
16|1|Passover, the Feast of Weeks and the Feast of Tabernacles; judges must be fair.
17|19|Laws for courts, and for a future king, who must read God's law all his life.
18|15|The priests' share; detestable practices forbidden; God will raise up a prophet like Moses.
19|9|Cities of refuge, boundary stones and honest witnesses.
20|4|Rules for war, trusting in the LORD who fights for Israel.
21|23|Unsolved murders, captive wives, the rights of the firstborn and a rebellious son.
22|1|Various laws about neighbours, kindness to animals and sexual purity.
23|5|Who may enter the assembly, cleanliness in the camp and honesty in vows.
24|19|Laws on divorce, loans, and leaving gleanings for the poor, the foreigner and the widow.
25|15|Fair punishment, levirate marriage, honest weights, and remembering Amalek.
26|2|Bringing the firstfruits with thanks; Israel is God's treasured possession.
27|10|Israel is to write the law on stones and proclaim curses for disobedience from Mount Ebal.
28|2|The blessings for obedience and the curses for disobedience, ending in exile.
29|29|The covenant renewed in Moab; the secret things belong to God, but the revealed things to us.
30|19|If Israel returns to God, he will restore them. Moses sets before them life and death: choose life.
31|6|Moses appoints Joshua and tells him to be strong and courageous. God warns that Israel will turn away.
32|4|The song of Moses: God is the Rock, faithful and just, though his people are unfaithful.
33|27|Moses blesses each tribe of Israel: the eternal God is your refuge.
34|10|Moses sees the promised land from Mount Nebo and dies. No prophet has arisen like him.
