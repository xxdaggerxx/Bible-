# Joshua
fits 1-12: Entering and conquering Canaan, about 1406–1400 BC
fits 13-24: Dividing the land, about 1400–1380 BC
1|9|After Moses dies, God tells Joshua to lead Israel into the land: be strong and courageous, for I am with you.
2|11|Joshua sends two spies to Jericho. Rahab hides them because she believes in Israel's God, and they promise to save her family.
3|17|The priests carry the ark into the Jordan, the river stops flowing, and Israel crosses on dry ground.
4|24|Twelve stones from the river are set up as a memorial of what God did.
5|15|The new generation is circumcised and keeps Passover; the manna stops. Joshua meets the commander of the LORD's army.
6|20|Israel marches around Jericho for seven days, and the walls fall. Rahab and her family are saved.
7|11|Achan secretly keeps plunder from Jericho, and Israel is defeated at Ai until his sin is dealt with.
8|1|Israel takes Ai. Joshua builds an altar on Mount Ebal and reads the law to all the people.
9|14|The Gibeonites trick Israel into a treaty because the leaders did not ask the LORD.
10|14|Five kings attack Gibeon. God fights for Israel, sending hailstones and making the sun stand still.
11|23|Joshua defeats the kings of the north, and the land has rest from war.
12|24|A list of the kings defeated on both sides of the Jordan.
13|1|Much land remains to be taken. The land east of the Jordan is given to Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh.
14|12|Caleb, now 85, asks for the hill country God promised him and is given Hebron.
15|13|The land given to the tribe of Judah; Caleb takes Hebron and Debir.
16|10|The land given to Ephraim, who do not drive out the Canaanites of Gezer.
17|18|The land given to the half tribe of Manasseh, including the daughters of Zelophehad.
18|3|The tabernacle is set up at Shiloh, and the land of Benjamin is surveyed and given.
19|51|The land given to Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali and Dan, and Joshua's town.
20|3|Six cities of refuge are set apart.
21|45|Towns are given to the Levites. Not one of God's good promises has failed.
22|5|The eastern tribes go home and build an altar by the Jordan, which nearly causes a war until they explain it.
23|14|Old Joshua urges the leaders to love the LORD and keep away from the nations' gods.
24|15|At Shechem Joshua renews the covenant: "As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD." Joshua dies.
