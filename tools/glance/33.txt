# Micah
fits 1-7: Micah prophesies to Judah and Israel, about 735–700 BC
1|3|The LORD comes to judge Samaria and Jerusalem for their idols.
2|1|Woe to those who plot evil and seize the land of the poor; God will gather a remnant.
3|8|Micah condemns rulers, prophets and priests who sell justice for money.
4|2|In the last days nations will come to God's house; swords will become ploughshares.
5|2|From Bethlehem will come a ruler over Israel, whose origins are from of old.
6|8|What does the LORD require? To do justice, to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God.
7|18|Micah waits for God, who pardons sin and delights in steadfast love.
