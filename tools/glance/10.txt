# 2 Samuel
fits 1-24: David's reign, about 1010–970 BC
1|26|David mourns for Saul and Jonathan with a lament.
2|4|David is anointed king over Judah at Hebron. War begins with Saul's family.
3|1|David grows stronger. Abner joins him but is murdered by Joab.
4|11|Saul's son Ish-bosheth is murdered, and David punishes the killers.
5|12|David becomes king over all Israel, captures Jerusalem and defeats the Philistines.
6|14|David brings the ark to Jerusalem, dancing before the LORD with all his might.
7|16|God promises David a house: his kingdom and throne will last for ever.
8|15|David's victories over the surrounding nations; he rules with justice.
9|7|David shows kindness to Jonathan's lame son Mephibosheth.
10|12|David defeats the Ammonites and the Arameans.
11|27|David commits adultery with Bathsheba and arranges the death of her husband Uriah.
12|13|The prophet Nathan confronts David with a parable. David repents, but the child dies. Solomon is born.
13|28|Amnon wrongs his sister Tamar, and her brother Absalom kills him and flees.
14|14|Joab arranges for Absalom to come home, and David forgives him.
15|14|Absalom wins the people's hearts and rebels. David flees Jerusalem.
16|12|Ziba deceives David, and Shimei curses him, but David leaves it to the LORD.
17|14|Absalom follows Hushai's advice instead of Ahithophel's, and David escapes across the Jordan.
18|33|Absalom is defeated and killed by Joab. David weeps: "O Absalom, my son!"
19|22|David returns to Jerusalem and shows mercy to those who opposed him.
20|1|Sheba leads a rebellion, which Joab ends.
21|14|A famine for Saul's sin against the Gibeonites; battles with the Philistine giants.
22|2|David's song of praise: the LORD is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer.
23|5|David's last words, and a list of his mighty men.
24|24|David counts the fighting men in pride, and a plague follows. He buys a threshing floor and offers sacrifices.
