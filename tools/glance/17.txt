# Esther
fits 1-10: The Jews in Persia under King Xerxes, about 483–473 BC
1|12|King Xerxes of Persia holds a great feast, and Queen Vashti refuses his command and is removed.
2|17|Esther, a Jewish orphan raised by her cousin Mordecai, becomes queen. Mordecai uncovers a plot against the king.
3|6|Haman is promoted, and when Mordecai will not bow to him, he plots to destroy all the Jews.
4|14|Mordecai urges Esther to go to the king: who knows whether you have come to the kingdom for such a time as this?
5|2|Esther goes to the king unbidden, and he welcomes her. Haman builds a gallows for Mordecai.
6|10|The sleepless king learns that Mordecai saved his life, and Haman must honour Mordecai.
7|3|At Esther's banquet she reveals Haman's plot, and Haman is hanged on his own gallows.
8|17|The king gives a new decree letting the Jews defend themselves, and there is joy.
9|22|The Jews overcome their enemies, and the feast of Purim is set to remember it.
10|3|Mordecai becomes second to the king and works for the good of his people.
