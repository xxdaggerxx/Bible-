# 2 Timothy
fits 1-4: Paul's last letter, to Timothy, from prison in Rome, about AD 67
1|7|God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power, love and self-control; guard the gospel.
2|15|Be strong in grace; a good soldier of Christ; rightly handle the word of truth.
3|16|Hard times will come in the last days; all Scripture is breathed out by God and useful.
4|7|Preach the word; Paul has fought the good fight and finished the race.
