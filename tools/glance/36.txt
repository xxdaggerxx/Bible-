# Zephaniah
fits 1-3: Zephaniah prophesies in the days of King Josiah, about 630 BC
1|14|The great day of the LORD is near, a day of judgment on Judah and the world.
2|3|Seek the LORD, all you humble; judgment on the nations around.
3|17|Jerusalem will be judged, then restored; the LORD will rejoice over his people with singing.
