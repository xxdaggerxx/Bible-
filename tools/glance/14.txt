# 2 Chronicles
fits 1-9: Solomon's reign, about 970–930 BC
fits 10-36: The kings of Judah, from the split of the kingdom to the exile, 930–586 BC
1|10|Solomon asks God for wisdom and knowledge, and God gives him riches and honour too.
2|5|Solomon prepares to build the temple with help from King Hiram of Tyre.
3|1|Solomon builds the temple on Mount Moriah.
4|1|The temple furnishings: the altar, the bronze sea, the lampstands and tables.
5|13|The ark is brought into the temple, the singers praise God, and the cloud of his glory fills it.
6|18|Solomon's prayer of dedication: can God really live on earth? Hear from heaven and forgive.
7|14|Fire falls from heaven. God promises: if my people humble themselves and pray, I will heal their land.
8|1|Solomon's building projects and his worship.
9|8|The Queen of Sheba visits, and Solomon's wealth and wisdom. Solomon dies.
10|15|Rehoboam rejects the people's request, and the northern tribes break away.
11|16|Rehoboam strengthens Judah, and faithful people from the north come to Jerusalem.
12|7|Rehoboam abandons the LORD, Egypt attacks, and the king humbles himself.
13|18|Abijah defeats Jeroboam's larger army because Judah relies on the LORD.
14|11|King Asa removes idols and relies on God to defeat a huge Cushite army.
15|2|The prophet Azariah encourages Asa, who leads Judah to seek God wholeheartedly.
16|9|Asa relies on Aram instead of God, and the seer Hanani rebukes him.
17|6|Jehoshaphat sends teachers through Judah with the Book of the Law, and God makes him strong.
18|13|Jehoshaphat joins Ahab in battle against the prophet Micaiah's warning, and Ahab dies.
19|7|Jehoshaphat appoints judges and tells them to judge in the fear of the LORD.
20|15|A great army attacks Judah. Jehoshaphat prays, and God fights for them as the singers praise.
21|7|Jehoram does evil, yet God keeps his promise to David.
22|10|Ahaziah reigns briefly; Athaliah seizes the throne, but Joash is hidden.
23|16|The priest Jehoiada crowns Joash and leads the people back to God.
24|20|Joash repairs the temple but later turns away and has the prophet Zechariah stoned.
25|2|Amaziah does right, but not wholeheartedly, and turns to idols.
26|16|Uzziah prospers while he seeks God, but pride leads him into the temple and he is struck with a skin disease.
27|6|Jotham grows strong because he walks steadily before the LORD.
28|22|Ahaz turns to other gods and shuts the temple doors.
29|36|Hezekiah reopens and cleanses the temple and restores worship.
30|9|Hezekiah invites all Israel, north and south, to a great Passover.
31|21|The people give generously and Hezekiah organises the priests and Levites.
32|8|Assyria's King Sennacherib attacks, and God saves Jerusalem in answer to Hezekiah and Isaiah's prayer.
33|13|Manasseh does great evil, is taken captive, humbles himself, and God restores him.
34|31|Josiah seeks God, finds the Book of the Law and renews the covenant.
35|18|Josiah keeps a great Passover, then dies in battle against Egypt.
36|23|Judah's last kings ignore God's prophets; Jerusalem falls. Seventy years later Cyrus lets the exiles return.
