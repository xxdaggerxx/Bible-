# 2 Peter
fits 1-3: Peter's last letter, shortly before his death, about AD 65–68
1|21|Add goodness, knowledge and love to your faith; Scripture came from God as men were carried along by the Spirit.
2|9|False teachers will come and be judged, but the Lord knows how to rescue the godly.
3|9|The Lord is not slow about his promise; he is patient, wanting all to repent. The day of the Lord will come.
