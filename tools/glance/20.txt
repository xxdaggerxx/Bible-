# Proverbs
fits 1-9: Wisdom: Solomon's teaching to his son, about 950 BC
fits 10-24: Wisdom: Solomon's short sayings and the sayings of the wise
fits 25-31: Wisdom: Solomon's sayings copied by Hezekiah's men, about 700 BC, and the words of Agur and Lemuel
1|7|Why the proverbs were written. The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge; do not follow sinners. Wisdom calls out in the streets.
2|6|Seek wisdom like hidden treasure, for the LORD gives wisdom, and it will keep you from evil.
3|5|Trust in the LORD with all your heart and do not lean on your own understanding; he will make your paths straight.
4|23|A father urges his son to get wisdom; guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.
5|21|A warning against adultery; enjoy the wife of your youth.
6|6|Warnings against laziness (go to the ant), deceit, and seven things the LORD hates.
7|25|A young man is led astray by an adulterous woman; do not let your heart turn to her ways.
8|35|Wisdom speaks: she was with God at creation, and whoever finds her finds life.
9|10|Wisdom and Folly both invite people to their houses; the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom.
10|22|Short sayings contrasting the wise and the foolish, the righteous and the wicked.
11|2|Sayings about honesty, pride, generosity and the righteous.
12|15|Sayings about the tongue, hard work and listening to advice.
13|20|Sayings about discipline, wealth and good company: walk with the wise and become wise.
14|12|Sayings about wise living; there is a way that seems right, but its end is death.
15|1|A gentle answer turns away wrath; sayings about words, the heart and the LORD's care.
16|9|People plan their way, but the LORD directs their steps. Pride goes before destruction.
17|17|Sayings about peace, friendship and foolishness: a friend loves at all times.
18|10|The name of the LORD is a strong tower; sayings about words and relationships.
19|21|Sayings about poverty, patience and God's purposes, which stand.
20|24|Sayings about wine, honesty, kings and trusting God to guide our steps.
21|1|The king's heart is in the LORD's hand; sayings about justice and pride.
22|6|A good name is better than riches; train up a child in the way he should go.
23|4|The sayings of the wise: on greed, discipline and the dangers of drink.
24|16|More sayings of the wise: do not envy the wicked; the righteous rise again.
25|21|Hezekiah's collection: humility before the king, and kindness to enemies.
26|11|Sayings about fools, the lazy and gossip.
27|17|Sayings about friendship and planning: as iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another.
28|13|Sayings about righteousness and confession: whoever confesses sin finds mercy.
29|25|Sayings about discipline, leaders and trust in the LORD.
30|5|The words of Agur: every word of God proves true; he is a shield.
31|30|King Lemuel's mother's advice, and the poem of the wife of noble character who fears the LORD.
