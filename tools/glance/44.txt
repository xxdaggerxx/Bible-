# Acts
fits 1-7: The early church in Jerusalem, about AD 30–34
fits 8-12: The gospel spreads to Samaria and the Gentiles, about AD 34–44
fits 13-21: Paul's missionary journeys, about AD 46–57
fits 22-28: Paul a prisoner, from Jerusalem to Rome, about AD 57–62
1|8|Jesus promises the Holy Spirit and ascends to heaven: you will be my witnesses to the ends of the earth. Matthias replaces Judas.
2|38|At Pentecost the Holy Spirit comes; Peter preaches and three thousand believe and are baptized. The believers share life together.
3|19|Peter and John heal a lame man at the temple gate in Jesus' name, and Peter calls the people to repent.
4|12|Peter and John are arrested and tell the council there is salvation in no other name. The church prays for boldness.
5|29|Ananias and Sapphira lie and die. The apostles are jailed and freed: we must obey God rather than men.
6|7|Seven men, including Stephen, are chosen to serve the widows. Stephen is arrested.
7|59|Stephen tells Israel's story and is stoned to death, praying for his killers; Saul approves.
8|35|Persecution scatters the church. Philip preaches in Samaria and explains Isaiah to an Ethiopian official.
9|15|Saul meets the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus, is blinded, believes and begins to preach. Peter heals and raises Tabitha.
10|34|God shows Peter that the gospel is for Gentiles too; the Spirit falls on Cornelius's household.
11|18|Peter explains to the church in Jerusalem; the church in Antioch grows, and believers are first called Christians.
12|7|Herod kills James and jails Peter, but an angel frees him. Herod dies.
13|2|The Spirit sends Barnabas and Saul (Paul) on their first journey; Paul preaches in Antioch of Pisidia.
14|22|Paul and Barnabas preach in Iconium, Lystra and Derbe, and strengthen the new churches.
15|11|The council in Jerusalem decides Gentiles are saved by grace without keeping the law of Moses. Paul and Barnabas part.
16|31|Paul's second journey: Timothy joins him; Lydia believes in Philippi; Paul and Silas are jailed and the jailer is saved.
17|28|Paul preaches in Thessalonica and Berea, and to the philosophers in Athens about the unknown God.
18|9|Paul stays in Corinth with Aquila and Priscilla; Apollos preaches in Ephesus.
19|20|Paul's third journey: two years in Ephesus; a riot breaks out over the goddess Artemis.
20|35|Paul travels through Greece and says farewell to the Ephesian elders.
21|13|Paul goes to Jerusalem though warned, and is seized in the temple.
22|15|Paul tells the crowd how he met Jesus; he is a Roman citizen.
23|11|Paul before the council; a plot to kill him; he is sent to Caesarea.
24|16|Paul defends himself before Governor Felix and is kept in prison two years.
25|11|Before Festus, Paul appeals to Caesar.
26|18|Paul tells King Agrippa his story and urges him to believe.
27|25|On the voyage to Rome a storm wrecks the ship, but all are saved as God promised.
28|31|On Malta Paul survives a snakebite and heals the sick. In Rome he preaches the kingdom of God openly.
