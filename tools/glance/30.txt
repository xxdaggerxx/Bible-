# Amos
fits 1-9: Amos the shepherd prophesies to Israel in a time of wealth and injustice, about 760 BC
1|2|God will judge the nations around Israel for their cruelty.
2|6|Judah and Israel will be judged too, for rejecting God's law and trampling the poor.
3|2|Israel was chosen by God, so it will be held to account.
4|12|Israel would not return to God despite many warnings: prepare to meet your God.
5|24|Seek the LORD and live. God hates empty feasts; let justice roll down like waters.
6|1|Woe to those at ease in Zion who live in luxury and do not grieve over the ruin of Joseph.
7|15|Visions of locusts, fire and a plumb line. The priest Amaziah orders Amos away, but Amos obeys God.
8|11|A basket of summer fruit: the end has come. There will be a famine of hearing the words of the LORD.
9|11|God will judge Israel, but will restore David's fallen tent and bring his people home.
