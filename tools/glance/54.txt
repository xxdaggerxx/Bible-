# 1 Timothy
fits 1-6: Paul's letter to Timothy, leading the church in Ephesus, about AD 62–64
1|15|Guard against false teaching; Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost.
2|5|Pray for everyone; there is one God and one mediator, the man Christ Jesus. Worship in the church.
3|15|Qualifications for overseers and deacons; the mystery of godliness.
4|12|Beware of false teachers; train yourself in godliness; let no one despise your youth.
5|8|Caring for widows, honouring elders and correcting sin.
6|10|Godliness with contentment is great gain; the love of money is a root of all evil; fight the good fight.
