# Judges
fits 1-21: The judges, before Israel had a king, about 1380–1050 BC
1|28|After Joshua, the tribes fight on but fail to drive out the Canaanites.
2|12|A new generation forgets the LORD. A cycle begins: Israel sins, is oppressed, cries out, and God raises up judges to save them.
3|9|The judges Othniel, Ehud, who kills the king of Moab, and Shamgar.
4|14|The prophetess Deborah sends Barak against Sisera, and Jael kills Sisera in her tent.
5|31|The song of Deborah and Barak praising God for the victory.
6|12|Midian oppresses Israel. The angel of the LORD calls Gideon, who tears down Baal's altar and tests God with a fleece.
7|7|God reduces Gideon's army to 300 men, who defeat Midian with trumpets and torches.
8|23|Gideon pursues the Midianite kings, refuses to be king, but makes an ephod that becomes a snare.
9|56|Gideon's son Abimelech kills his brothers and makes himself king, then dies in his evil.
10|16|The judges Tola and Jair; Israel turns to idols again, and the Ammonites oppress them.
11|30|Jephthah, an outcast, leads Israel against Ammon and makes a rash vow about his daughter.
12|6|Jephthah fights Ephraim. The judges Ibzan, Elon and Abdon.
13|5|The angel of the LORD announces the birth of Samson, who is to be a Nazirite.
14|6|Samson kills a lion, marries a Philistine woman, and tells a riddle that leads to trouble.
15|15|Samson takes revenge on the Philistines and kills a thousand with a donkey's jawbone.
16|28|Delilah betrays Samson, who is blinded. In his death he pulls down the temple of Dagon on the Philistines.
17|6|Micah makes an idol and hires a Levite as his priest. Everyone did what was right in his own eyes.
18|1|The tribe of Dan takes Micah's idol and priest and conquers the town of Laish.
19|30|A Levite's concubine is abused and killed at Gibeah in Benjamin, a crime that shocks Israel.
20|28|Israel goes to war against Benjamin, which is almost wiped out.
21|25|Israel finds wives for the surviving Benjamites. There was no king in Israel.
