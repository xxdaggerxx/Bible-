# 1 Chronicles
fits 1-9: Family records from Adam to the return from exile, written after the exile
fits 10-29: David's reign, about 1010–970 BC
1|1|Family records from Adam through Noah's sons to Abraham, Isaac and Esau.
2|1|The sons of Israel and the family of Judah, leading to David.
3|1|The family of David and the kings of Judah who came from him.
4|10|More of the family of Judah, including Jabez's prayer, and the family of Simeon.
5|20|The tribes east of the Jordan: Reuben, Gad and half of Manasseh, who trusted God in battle but later went into exile.
6|31|The family of Levi, the singers David appointed, and the Levites' towns.
7|40|The families of Issachar, Benjamin, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim and Asher.
8|33|The family of Benjamin, including the family of Saul.
9|2|The people who returned to Jerusalem after the exile, and the family of Saul.
10|13|Saul dies in battle with the Philistines because he was unfaithful to the LORD.
11|9|David is made king of all Israel and takes Jerusalem. His mighty men.
12|18|The warriors who came to David, and the great gathering that made him king.
13|14|David's first attempt to bring the ark to Jerusalem ends when Uzzah dies.
14|17|David's household grows and he defeats the Philistines.
15|15|The Levites carry the ark to Jerusalem as God commanded, with music and joy.
16|34|David's song of thanks when the ark arrives: give thanks to the LORD, for he is good.
17|14|God promises David a house and a kingdom that will last for ever. David's prayer of thanks.
18|14|David's victories over the surrounding nations.
19|13|The Ammonites insult David's messengers, and David defeats them and the Arameans.
20|8|The capture of Rabbah, and the killing of Philistine giants.
21|13|David counts the people, a plague follows, and he buys the threshing floor where the temple will stand.
22|19|David prepares materials for the temple and charges Solomon to build it.
23|30|David organises the Levites for their service.
24|31|The priests are divided into twenty-four groups.
25|1|The musicians appointed to sing and prophesy with instruments.
26|1|The gatekeepers, treasurers and other officials.
27|1|The army divisions and leaders of the tribes.
28|9|David gives Solomon the plans for the temple and urges him to serve God with a whole heart.
29|11|The people give generously for the temple. David praises God and Solomon becomes king.
