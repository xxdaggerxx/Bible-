# 1 Corinthians
fits 1-16: Paul's letter to the church in Corinth, about AD 55
1|18|Paul urges unity; the message of the cross is foolishness to some but God's power to us.
2|2|Paul came preaching Christ crucified, in the Spirit's power, not clever words.
3|11|Workers together in God's field; Christ is the only foundation; you are God's temple.
4|2|Servants of Christ must be faithful; Paul's example of humble service.
5|7|The church must deal with sexual sin in its midst; Christ our Passover lamb is sacrificed.
6|19|Do not take believers to court; flee sexual sin; your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit.
7|17|Teaching on marriage, singleness and living as God has called you.
8|1|Food offered to idols: knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.
9|22|Paul gives up his rights for the gospel and runs to win the prize.
10|13|Lessons from Israel; God will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear; do all to God's glory.
11|24|Head coverings, and the right way to share the Lord's Supper.
12|27|Spiritual gifts; one body with many parts.
13|13|Love is patient and kind; faith, hope and love remain, and the greatest is love.
14|40|Prophecy and tongues; everything done for building up and in order.
15|20|Christ has been raised, the firstfruits; the resurrection body; death is swallowed up in victory.
16|14|The collection for Jerusalem, travel plans and greetings; let all be done in love.
