# 2 Corinthians
fits 1-13: Paul's second letter to the church in Corinth, about AD 56
1|4|God comforts us in all our troubles so we can comfort others.
2|14|Paul forgives the offender and gives thanks for spreading the fragrance of Christ.
3|18|The new covenant of the Spirit is more glorious than the old; we are being transformed.
4|7|We have this treasure in jars of clay; we do not lose heart.
5|17|We long for our heavenly home. If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; be reconciled to God.
6|2|Now is the day of salvation; Paul's hardships; do not be yoked with unbelievers.
7|10|Paul rejoices that his letter led them to godly sorrow and repentance.
8|9|The example of the Macedonians' giving; Christ became poor so you might become rich.
9|7|God loves a cheerful giver; he is able to make all grace abound.
10|5|Paul defends his ministry: take every thought captive to Christ.
11|30|Paul answers the false apostles by boasting of his sufferings.
12|9|Paul's thorn in the flesh: my grace is sufficient for you, for power is made perfect in weakness.
13|5|Examine yourselves; Paul's final warnings and blessing.
