# Nehemiah
fits 1-13: Rebuilding Jerusalem's walls and renewing the covenant, 445–430 BC
1|11|Nehemiah, the Persian king's cupbearer, hears that Jerusalem's walls are broken down, and he weeps and prays.
2|18|King Artaxerxes sends Nehemiah to rebuild Jerusalem. He inspects the walls at night and rallies the people.
3|1|The families and groups who rebuilt each section of the wall and gates.
4|14|Enemies mock and threaten, so the builders work with a tool in one hand and a weapon in the other.
5|9|Nehemiah stops the rich from oppressing poor Jews and refuses his own food allowance.
6|15|Enemies try to trick and frighten Nehemiah, but the wall is finished in fifty-two days.
7|2|Nehemiah appoints guards and lists the families who first returned.
8|10|Ezra reads the Book of the Law to all the people; they weep, then celebrate. The joy of the LORD is your strength.
9|17|The Levites lead a long prayer recalling God's faithfulness and Israel's sin, and the people make a binding agreement.
10|29|The leaders sign the agreement to keep God's law, the Sabbath and the offerings.
11|1|One in ten families moves to live in Jerusalem.
12|43|The priests and Levites, and the joyful dedication of the wall.
13|14|Returning later, Nehemiah finds the people breaking their promises and puts things right again.
