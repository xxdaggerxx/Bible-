# Malachi
fits 1-4: Malachi, the last Old Testament prophet, about 430 BC
1|2|God has loved Israel, but the priests offer him blemished sacrifices.
2|16|The priests have failed to teach truly, and the people are unfaithful in marriage.
3|10|God will send his messenger; I the LORD do not change. Bring the full tithe and see if I will not open heaven's windows.
4|5|The day is coming when the sun of righteousness will rise; God will send Elijah before it.
