# Ezra
fits 1-6: The first return from exile and the rebuilt temple, 538–516 BC
fits 7-10: Ezra returns to teach God's law, 458 BC
1|3|King Cyrus of Persia lets the Jews go home to rebuild the temple, as God had stirred his heart.
2|1|A list of the families who returned to Jerusalem and Judah.
3|11|The returned exiles rebuild the altar and lay the temple's foundation with praise and weeping.
4|4|Enemies oppose the rebuilding and get the work stopped.
5|2|The prophets Haggai and Zechariah encourage the people, and the work starts again.
6|16|King Darius confirms Cyrus's decree. The temple is finished and dedicated with joy, and Passover is kept.
7|10|Ezra, a teacher of God's law, comes to Jerusalem; he had set his heart to study, obey and teach it.
8|23|Ezra leads another group home, fasting and praying for God's protection.
9|6|Ezra is grieved that the people have married foreign wives who worship other gods, and he confesses Israel's sin.
10|11|The people confess and agree to put things right.
