# Habakkuk
fits 1-3: Habakkuk questions God as Babylon rises, about 605 BC
1|2|Habakkuk asks why God allows injustice; God says he is raising up the Babylonians, and Habakkuk asks how that can be right.
2|4|God answers: write the vision; the righteous shall live by his faith. Woes on the proud oppressor.
3|18|Habakkuk's prayer: though the fig tree does not blossom, yet I will rejoice in the LORD.
