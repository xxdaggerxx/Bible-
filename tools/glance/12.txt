# 2 Kings
fits 1-13: Elisha and the kings of Israel and Judah, about 853–796 BC
fits 14-17: The fall of the northern kingdom of Israel to Assyria, 722 BC
fits 18-25: Judah alone until the exile to Babylon, 715–586 BC
1|16|King Ahaziah of Israel seeks a foreign god, and Elijah says he will die.
2|11|Elijah is taken up to heaven in a whirlwind, and Elisha receives a double portion of his spirit.
3|18|Elisha prophesies water and victory over Moab.
4|7|Elisha's miracles: oil for a widow, a son for a Shunammite woman raised to life, and food for prophets.
5|14|Naaman, an Aramean commander, is healed of his skin disease by washing in the Jordan. Gehazi's greed.
6|17|An axe head floats. Elisha's servant sees the hills full of horses and chariots of fire. Samaria is besieged.
7|1|God ends the siege of Samaria as Elisha said, and four lepers find the enemy camp empty.
8|1|The Shunammite's land is restored. Hazael becomes king of Aram. Kings of Judah.
9|6|Jehu is anointed king of Israel and kills Joram, Ahaziah and Jezebel.
10|30|Jehu destroys Ahab's family and the worship of Baal, but keeps Jeroboam's calves.
11|17|Athaliah seizes Judah's throne, but the boy Joash is hidden and later crowned.
12|2|Joash does right while the priest Jehoiada teaches him, and he repairs the temple.
13|23|Kings of Israel. Elisha dies, and a dead man comes to life when he touches Elisha's bones.
14|3|Amaziah of Judah and Jeroboam II of Israel.
15|35|A series of kings in Judah and Israel; Assyria begins to threaten Israel.
16|3|King Ahaz of Judah follows pagan ways and copies an Assyrian altar.
17|7|Assyria captures Samaria and carries Israel into exile because they rejected the LORD and his covenant.
18|5|Hezekiah of Judah trusts the LORD and removes idols. The Assyrian commander mocks God at Jerusalem.
19|19|Hezekiah prays, Isaiah gives God's answer, and the angel of the LORD strikes the Assyrian army.
20|5|God heals Hezekiah and adds fifteen years to his life. Hezekiah shows his treasures to Babylon.
21|2|Manasseh leads Judah into great evil, and God announces disaster on Jerusalem.
22|13|In King Josiah's day the Book of the Law is found in the temple, and Josiah is troubled by it.
23|25|Josiah renews the covenant, removes idols and keeps the Passover. He dies in battle.
24|20|Babylon's King Nebuchadnezzar takes Jerusalem and carries King Jehoiachin and many people to Babylon.
25|9|Jerusalem and the temple are destroyed, and Judah is taken into exile. Jehoiachin is later shown kindness.
