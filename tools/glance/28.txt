# Hosea
fits 1-14: Hosea prophesies to the northern kingdom of Israel before it falls, about 755–715 BC
1|10|God tells Hosea to marry Gomer, who will be unfaithful, as a picture of Israel's unfaithfulness. Their children's names carry God's message.
2|19|Israel has chased other gods like an unfaithful wife, but God will win her back and betroth her to himself for ever.
3|1|Hosea buys back his unfaithful wife, as God loves Israel though they turn to other gods.
4|6|God brings charges against Israel: there is no knowledge of God in the land, and the people are destroyed for lack of it.
5|15|Judgment on Israel's priests and leaders; God will wait until they seek his face.
6|6|Come, let us return to the LORD; God desires steadfast love, not sacrifice.
7|10|Israel is like a half-baked cake and a silly dove, turning everywhere but to God.
8|7|They sow the wind and reap the whirlwind; Israel has made idols and kings without God.
9|17|Israel's joy will end in exile because they rejected God.
10|12|Sow righteousness and reap steadfast love; break up your fallow ground and seek the LORD.
11|1|When Israel was a child I loved him and called my son out of Egypt; God's love cannot give them up.
12|6|Like Jacob, return to God, hold fast to love and justice, and wait for him.
13|14|Israel will be judged for its idols, yet God will ransom them from death.
14|4|Return to the LORD with words of repentance; God will heal their faithlessness and love them freely.
