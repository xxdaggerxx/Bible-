# Jonah
fits 1-4: Jonah and the city of Nineveh, about 760 BC
1|17|God sends Jonah to warn Nineveh, but he runs the other way. In a storm he is thrown overboard and swallowed by a great fish.
2|9|Jonah prays from inside the fish: salvation belongs to the LORD. The fish spits him out on dry land.
3|10|Jonah preaches in Nineveh, the people repent, and God spares the city.
4|11|Jonah is angry that God showed mercy. God uses a plant to show his compassion for Nineveh.
