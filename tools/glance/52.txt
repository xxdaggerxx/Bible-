# 1 Thessalonians
fits 1-5: Paul's first letter to the church in Thessalonica, about AD 50–51
1|9|Paul thanks God for their faith, love and hope; they turned from idols to serve the living God.
2|13|Paul recalls his gentle care among them; they received God's word as truly God's word.
3|12|Timothy's good news of their faith; Paul prays for their love to increase.
4|16|Live to please God in holiness; the Lord will come, and the dead in Christ will rise first.
5|18|Be ready for the day of the Lord; rejoice always, pray continually, give thanks in all circumstances.
