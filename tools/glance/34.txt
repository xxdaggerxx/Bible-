# Nahum
fits 1-3: Nahum foretells the fall of Nineveh, capital of Assyria (fell in 612 BC)
1|7|The LORD is slow to anger but will judge his enemies; he is a refuge in the day of trouble.
2|13|A vivid picture of Nineveh's attack and fall.
3|19|Woe to the city of blood; Nineveh's ruin is certain and no one will mourn.
