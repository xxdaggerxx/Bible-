# Exodus
fits 1-18: Out of Egypt, about 1446 BC
fits 19-40: At Mount Sinai, about 1446–1445 BC
1|12|A new Pharaoh fears the growing Israelites and makes them slaves. When they keep growing, he orders their baby boys killed, but the midwives fear God.
2|24|Moses is born and hidden in a basket, then raised by Pharaoh's daughter. He kills an Egyptian, flees to Midian and marries. God hears Israel's groaning.
3|14|God speaks to Moses from a burning bush and sends him to free Israel. He reveals his name: "I AM WHO I AM."
4|12|Moses makes excuses, and God gives him signs and his brother Aaron to speak for him. Moses returns to Egypt, and the people believe.
5|2|Moses and Aaron ask Pharaoh to let the people go, and Pharaoh refuses and makes their work harder. The people blame Moses.
6|7|God promises to bring Israel out with power and make them his people. The family line of Moses and Aaron.
7|5|Aaron's staff becomes a snake, and the first plague turns the Nile to blood. Pharaoh's heart is hard.
8|19|The plagues of frogs, gnats and flies. Pharaoh promises to let the people go, then changes his mind.
9|16|The plagues of disease on the livestock, boils and hail. God says he raised Pharaoh up to show his power in all the earth.
10|2|The plagues of locusts and darkness. Pharaoh still refuses and drives Moses out.
11|7|Moses warns of the last plague: the death of every firstborn in Egypt.
12|13|God gives the Passover: each family kills a lamb and puts its blood on the doorposts so the LORD passes over them. The firstborn of Egypt die, and Israel leaves.
13|21|The firstborn are to be set apart to the LORD. God leads his people by a pillar of cloud by day and fire by night.
14|14|Pharaoh chases Israel to the Red Sea. God parts the sea, Israel crosses on dry ground, and the Egyptian army drowns.
15|2|Moses and Israel sing praise to the LORD for his victory. At Marah God makes bitter water sweet.
16|4|The people grumble for food, and God sends quail and manna from heaven each morning, with a double portion before the Sabbath.
17|6|God gives water from the rock at Horeb. Israel defeats the Amalekites while Moses holds up his hands.
18|21|Moses' father-in-law Jethro visits and advises him to appoint leaders to help judge the people.
19|5|Israel camps at Mount Sinai. God offers to make them his treasured people, and comes down on the mountain in fire and thunder.
20|3|God gives the Ten Commandments. The people are afraid, and Moses draws near to God.
21|23|Laws for Hebrew servants and for injuries and violence, including "eye for eye".
22|21|Laws about property, care for the weak, and holiness.
23|9|Laws about justice, the Sabbath year and the three yearly feasts. God promises to send his angel ahead of them.
24|7|The people promise to obey, and the covenant is sealed with blood. Moses goes up the mountain for forty days.
25|8|God gives the plans for the tabernacle, so he may live among his people: the ark, the table and the lampstand.
26|33|Plans for the tabernacle's curtains, frames and the veil before the Most Holy Place.
27|20|Plans for the bronze altar, the courtyard and oil for the lamp.
28|29|The holy garments for Aaron and his sons, the priests, with the names of Israel's tribes on the high priest's breastpiece.
29|45|How the priests are to be set apart, and the daily offerings. God will dwell among Israel and be their God.
30|10|The altar of incense, the atonement money, the bronze basin, the anointing oil and incense.
31|13|God fills Bezalel with his Spirit to build the tabernacle. The Sabbath is a sign between God and Israel. God gives the two tablets.
32|26|While Moses is on the mountain the people make a golden calf. Moses breaks the tablets and pleads with God for the people.
33|14|God says he will not go with the people, then agrees when Moses pleads. Moses asks to see his glory.
34|6|God gives new tablets and proclaims his name: merciful and gracious, slow to anger. Moses' face shines.
35|21|Moses gathers the people's willing gifts for the tabernacle.
36|5|The people bring more than enough, and the skilled workers make the curtains and frames.
37|1|Bezalel makes the ark, the table, the lampstand and the altar of incense.
38|1|They make the altar of burnt offering, the basin and the courtyard. An account of the metals used.
39|43|The priests' garments are made, and the finished work is brought to Moses, who blesses the people.
40|34|The tabernacle is set up, and the glory of the LORD fills it. The cloud leads Israel on their journeys.
