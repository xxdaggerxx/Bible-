# Isaiah to Malachi

== Isaiah saw his glory
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 6:1-3
nt: John 12:41
Isaiah saw the Lord on his throne, high and lifted up, and heard "Holy, holy, holy". John says, "These things said Esaias, when he saw his glory, and spake of him": of Jesus.

== A virgin shall conceive: Immanuel
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 7:14
nt: Matt 1:22-23; Luke 1:26-35
"A virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel", God with us. Matthew says Jesus' birth of the virgin Mary fulfilled it.

== A stone of stumbling
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 8:14
nt: Rom 9:32-33; 1Pet 2:7-8
The LORD would be a sanctuary, but a stone of stumbling to both houses of Israel. Paul and Peter say Christ is this stone, a refuge to those who trust and a stumbling block to those who refuse.

== A great light in Galilee
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 9:1-2
nt: Matt 4:12-17
The people of Galilee of the nations, who walked in darkness, have seen a great light. Matthew says this was fulfilled when Jesus began to preach there.

== Unto us a child is born
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 9:6-7
nt: Luke 1:32-33; Luke 2:11; John 1:1,14
A child born, a son given, called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace, on David's throne for ever. Fulfilled in Jesus.

== A rod out of the stem of Jesse
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 11:1-5,10
nt: Rom 15:12; Rev 5:5; Rev 22:16; Matt 3:16; Acts 13:22-23
A shoot from the stump of Jesse (David's father), with the Spirit of the LORD resting on him, judging in righteousness; the Gentiles shall seek him. Fulfilled in Christ, the root and offspring of David.

== The key of David
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 22:22
nt: Rev 3:7
"The key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut." Jesus applies it to himself: "he that hath the key of David".

== Death swallowed up in victory
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 25:8
nt: 1Cor 15:54-57; Rev 21:4
"He will swallow up death in victory; and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from off all faces." Fulfilled in Christ's resurrection and, in full, at his return.

== A sure foundation in Zion
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 28:16
nt: Rom 9:33; Rom 10:11; 1Pet 2:4-6; Eph 2:20
A tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation; "he that believeth shall not make haste". Paul and Peter say this stone is Christ.

== The blind see, the lame leap
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 35:4-6
nt: Matt 11:2-6; Luke 7:20-22
"Your God will come... Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened." When John the Baptist asked if Jesus was the one, Jesus pointed to these very signs.

== Prepare ye the way of the LORD
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 40:3-5
nt: Matt 3:1-3; John 1:23; Luke 3:4-6
A voice in the wilderness prepares the way of the LORD, and all flesh shall see his glory. John the Baptist was that voice; the LORD whose way he prepared was Jesus.

== He shall feed his flock like a shepherd
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 40:9-11
nt: John 10:11-16; Heb 13:20; 1Pet 5:4
"Behold your God!" He comes with strength, and feeds his flock like a shepherd, carrying the lambs. Jesus, the good shepherd, is God come to his people.

== My servant, in whom my soul delighteth
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 42:1-4
nt: Matt 12:15-21; Matt 3:16-17
God's chosen servant, with his Spirit on him, gentle (he will not break a bruised reed), bringing justice to the Gentiles. Matthew says this is Jesus.

== A light of the Gentiles
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 42:6-7; Isa 49:6
nt: Luke 2:30-32; Acts 13:47; Acts 26:23; John 8:12
The servant is a covenant for the people and a light to the nations, opening blind eyes, God's salvation to the end of the earth. Simeon held the baby Jesus and called him "a light to lighten the Gentiles".

== Every knee shall bow
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 45:22-23
nt: Phil 2:9-11; Rom 14:10-11
"Unto me every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear." Paul applies these words, spoken by the LORD, to Jesus: every knee shall bow at his name.

== I gave my back to the smiters
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 50:6
nt: Matt 26:67; Matt 27:26-30; John 19:1-3
"I gave my back to the smiters, and my cheeks to them that plucked off the hair: I hid not my face from shame and spitting." So Jesus was beaten, spat on and scourged.

== My servant shall be exalted
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 52:13-15
nt: Phil 2:7-11; Rom 15:21; Acts 3:13
The servant will be lifted up very high, after his face was marred more than any man's, and he shall sprinkle many nations. The start of Isaiah's song of the suffering servant.

== Despised and rejected of men
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 53:1-3
nt: John 12:37-38; Rom 10:16; John 1:10-11; Mark 9:12
Who has believed? He grew up with no beauty to draw us; despised and rejected, a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief. So Jesus was rejected by his own.

== Wounded for our transgressions
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 53:4-6
nt: Matt 8:16-17; 1Pet 2:24-25; Rom 4:25; 2Cor 5:21
"He was wounded for our transgressions... with his stripes we are healed... the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all." The heart of the gospel: Christ died in our place.

== As a lamb to the slaughter
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 53:7-8
nt: Acts 8:30-35; Matt 26:62-63; Matt 27:12-14; 1Pet 2:23
Oppressed and afflicted, he opened not his mouth, led as a lamb to the slaughter, cut off for the transgression of my people. Philip began at this scripture and preached Jesus.

== With the rich in his death
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 53:9
nt: Matt 27:57-60; 1Pet 2:22
He made his grave with the wicked, and with the rich in his death, though he had done no violence. Jesus died between thieves and was buried in a rich man's tomb.

== He shall see his seed
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 53:10-12
nt: Luke 22:37; Mark 15:27-28; Heb 7:25; Heb 9:28; Rom 5:19
An offering for sin, yet he shall prolong his days and see his seed; he bore the sin of many, was numbered with the transgressors, and made intercession for them. His death, resurrection and prayer for us.

== The sure mercies of David
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 55:3-4
nt: Acts 13:34
"I will make an everlasting covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David." Paul preached that God gave these by raising Jesus from the dead, no more to return to corruption.

== The Redeemer shall come to Zion
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 59:20
nt: Rom 11:26-27
"The Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob." Paul quotes it of Christ: "There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer."

== The Spirit of the Lord is upon me
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 61:1-2
nt: Luke 4:16-21
Anointed to preach good tidings to the meek, bind up the brokenhearted, proclaim liberty to the captives. Jesus read this in the synagogue at Nazareth and said, "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears."

== Behold, thy salvation cometh
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 62:11
nt: Matt 21:4-5
"Say ye to the daughter of Zion, Behold, thy salvation cometh." Matthew joins this to Zechariah's words when Jesus rides into Jerusalem.

== Treading the winepress alone
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 63:1-3
nt: Rev 19:13-15
One comes in garments red from treading the winepress of God's wrath alone. Revelation shows Christ, in a vesture dipped in blood, treading the winepress of the wrath of God.

== The righteous Branch: The LORD our righteousness
kind: prophecy
refs: Jer 23:5-6; Jer 33:15-16
nt: Luke 1:31-33; 1Cor 1:30; Rom 3:22
A righteous Branch raised up to David, a King who shall reign and prosper, whose name is THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS. Christ is made unto us righteousness.

== Rachel weeping for her children
kind: prophecy
refs: Jer 31:15
nt: Matt 2:16-18
"Rachel weeping for her children refused to be comforted." Matthew says this was fulfilled when Herod killed the boys of Bethlehem, trying to kill the infant Jesus.

== A new covenant
kind: prophecy
refs: Jer 31:31-34
nt: Luke 22:20; Heb 8:6-13; Heb 10:15-18
God promised a new covenant: his law written on the heart, and sins remembered no more. At the Last Supper Jesus said, "This cup is the new testament in my blood."

== One shepherd, my servant David
kind: prophecy
refs: Ezek 34:23-24; Ezek 37:24-25
nt: John 10:11,14-16; Heb 13:20; Luke 1:32
God will set up one shepherd over his flock, "my servant David", their prince for ever. Written long after David died: fulfilled in Jesus, the Son of David and the good shepherd.

== The stone cut without hands
kind: prophecy
refs: Dan 2:34-35,44-45
nt: Luke 20:17-18; Rev 11:15
A stone cut out without hands breaks the kingdoms of the world and becomes a mountain filling the earth: God's kingdom that shall never be destroyed. The kingdom of Christ.

== One like the Son of man
kind: prophecy
refs: Dan 7:13-14
nt: Matt 26:63-64; Mark 14:61-62; Rev 1:7,13
One like the Son of man comes with the clouds of heaven and is given an everlasting kingdom. "Son of man" was Jesus' own name for himself, and he quoted this at his trial.

== Messiah the Prince cut off
kind: prophecy
refs: Dan 9:24-26
nt: Gal 4:4; Mark 1:15; Heb 9:26-28
Seventy weeks to finish transgression, make reconciliation for iniquity and bring in everlasting righteousness; Messiah the Prince shall be cut off, but not for himself. Traditionally read of the time of Christ's coming and death.

== On the third day
kind: prophecy
refs: Hos 6:2
nt: 1Cor 15:3-4; Luke 24:46
"After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up." Traditionally read of Christ, raised the third day "according to the scriptures".

== Out of Egypt have I called my son
kind: prophecy
refs: Hos 11:1
nt: Matt 2:13-15
God called Israel, his son, out of Egypt. Matthew sees it fulfilled in Jesus, the true Son, brought back from Egypt as a child.

== O death, where is thy sting?
kind: prophecy
refs: Hos 13:14
nt: 1Cor 15:54-57
"I will ransom them from the power of the grave... O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction." Paul's song of Christ's victory over death.

== Whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD
kind: prophecy
refs: Joel 2:32
nt: Rom 10:9-13; Acts 2:21,36
Whoever calls on the name of the LORD shall be delivered. Peter and Paul apply it to calling on the name of the Lord Jesus.

== The tabernacle of David raised
kind: prophecy
refs: Amos 9:11-12
nt: Acts 15:13-18
God will raise up the fallen tabernacle of David, and the nations will be called by his name. James said this is fulfilled as Gentiles come to Christ.

== Three days in the fish
kind: type
refs: Jonah 1:17
nt: Matt 12:39-41; Luke 11:29-30
"As Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth." Jesus' sign of his death and resurrection.

== Out of Bethlehem
kind: prophecy
refs: Mic 5:2,4
nt: Matt 2:1-6; John 7:42
"But thou, Bethlehem... out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting." The scribes told Herod the Christ would be born there.

== The Branch, priest upon his throne
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 3:8; Zech 6:12-13
nt: Heb 8:1; Heb 7:24-26; Eph 2:20-22
"My servant the BRANCH" shall build the temple of the LORD and be a priest upon his throne. Christ is both King and Priest, building God's temple, his people.

== Thy King cometh, riding upon an ass
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 9:9
nt: Matt 21:1-9; John 12:12-16
"Behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass." Jesus rode into Jerusalem this way to fulfil it.

== Thirty pieces of silver
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 11:12-13
nt: Matt 26:14-15; Matt 27:3-10
The shepherd was valued at thirty pieces of silver, cast to the potter in the house of the LORD. Judas was paid thirty pieces of silver; it went to buy the potter's field.

== They shall look upon me whom they have pierced
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 12:10
nt: John 19:34-37; Rev 1:7
"They shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and they shall mourn for him." John saw the soldier pierce Jesus' side and wrote that this scripture was fulfilled.

== Smite the shepherd
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 13:7
nt: Matt 26:31,56; Mark 14:27,50
"Smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered." Jesus quoted it on the night of his arrest, and all the disciples fled.

== The Lord shall suddenly come to his temple
kind: prophecy
refs: Mal 3:1
nt: Matt 11:10; Mark 1:2; Luke 2:22-32; Luke 7:27
God sends his messenger to prepare the way, and the Lord comes suddenly to his temple. Jesus said John was that messenger; Jesus himself came to the temple.

== The Sun of righteousness
kind: prophecy
refs: Mal 4:2
nt: Luke 1:76-79; John 8:12
"Unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings." Zacharias sang of Christ as the dayspring from on high.

== Elijah the forerunner
kind: prophecy
refs: Mal 4:5-6
nt: Matt 11:13-14; Matt 17:10-13; Luke 1:17
Elijah will come before the great day of the LORD. Jesus said John the Baptist was the Elijah who was to come, going before the Lord Jesus.

== A throne established in mercy
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 16:5
nt: Luke 1:31-33
"In mercy shall the throne be established: and he shall sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of David, judging." The angel told Mary her son would sit on David's throne.

== A king shall reign in righteousness
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 32:1-2
nt: Heb 1:8-9; Matt 11:28-29
A king reigns in righteousness, and a man is a hiding place from the wind and a shelter from the storm. Traditionally read of Christ, the righteous King who gives rest.

== The first and the last
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 44:6; Isa 48:12
nt: Rev 1:17-18; Rev 22:13
"I am the first, and I am the last; and beside me there is no God." In Revelation the risen Jesus says the same of himself: "I am the first and the last."

== Called and named from the womb
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 49:1-3,5,7
nt: Matt 1:21; Luke 1:31; John 1:11; Phil 2:9-10
The servant was called from the womb and named before birth, despised by the nation, yet kings shall rise and worship. Jesus was named by the angel before he was born.

== The tongue of the learned
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 50:4-5,7
nt: John 8:28-29; John 12:49-50; Luke 9:51
The servant speaks a word in season to the weary, his ear opened each morning; he sets his face like a flint. Jesus spoke only what the Father taught him and set his face to go to Jerusalem.

== Gentiles come to thy light
kind: prophecy
refs: Isa 60:1-3,6
nt: Matt 2:1-11; Luke 2:32; John 8:12
"The Gentiles shall come to thy light, and kings to the brightness of thy rising"; they bring gold and incense. The wise men brought gold and frankincense to Jesus.

== David their king
kind: prophecy
refs: Jer 30:9; Hos 3:5
nt: Luke 1:31-33; Acts 13:22-23
They shall serve the LORD their God, "and David their king, whom I will raise up unto them". Written long after David's death: fulfilled in Christ, the Son of David.

== Until he come whose right it is
kind: prophecy
refs: Ezek 21:26-27
nt: Luke 1:31-33; Rev 19:16
The crown is taken away and overturned "until he come whose right it is; and I will give it him". Traditionally read of Christ, the rightful King.

== The tender twig on the high mountain
kind: prophecy
refs: Ezek 17:22-24
nt: Matt 13:31-32; Luke 1:32-33
God takes a tender twig from the top of the cedar and plants it on a high mountain, and it becomes a goodly cedar sheltering every bird. Traditionally read of Christ, the shoot of David's line, and his kingdom.

== The desire of all nations
kind: prophecy
refs: Hag 2:6-7,9
nt: Heb 12:26-28; Luke 2:27-32; John 2:19-21
"The desire of all nations shall come: and I will fill this house with glory... The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former." Christ came to that temple.

== I will dwell in the midst of thee
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 2:10-11
nt: John 1:14; Rev 21:3
"Lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the LORD", and many nations shall join themselves to him. God came to dwell among us in Christ.

== A fountain opened for sin
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 13:1
nt: John 19:34; 1John 1:7; Rev 1:5
"In that day there shall be a fountain opened... for sin and for uncleanness." Blood and water flowed from Jesus' side; his blood cleanses from all sin.

== His feet upon the mount of Olives
kind: prophecy
refs: Zech 14:4,9
nt: Acts 1:9-12; Rev 11:15
"His feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives", and the LORD shall be king over all the earth. Jesus ascended from the mount of Olives, and the angels said he would come again in like manner.
