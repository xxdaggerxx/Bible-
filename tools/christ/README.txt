Points to Christ (AID-13): Old Testament verses that point to Jesus Christ, in the traditional view.

Written by AI (Claude, in the 2.8 build session) and checked by tools/build_christ.py, which writes
app/src/main/assets/study/christ.tsv.

Each entry:

    == Title (short, shown in bold on the card)
    kind: prophecy | type
    refs: the Old Testament verses to mark, e.g. Gen 22:2,6-8,13-14; Isa 52:13-53:12
    nt: where the New Testament fulfils or explains it, e.g. Matt 2:4-6; John 7:42
    The note: one or two plain sentences.

prophecy: a promise or prediction the New Testament says Jesus fulfilled, or one traditionally read
as about the Messiah. type: a person, event or thing that pictures Christ. Book names are OSIS
(Gen, Exod, Ps, Isa, Matt, 1Cor ...). Verse numbers are the KJV's.
