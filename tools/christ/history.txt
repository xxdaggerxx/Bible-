# Joshua to Esther, and Job

== Boaz the kinsman-redeemer
kind: type
refs: Ruth 3:9; Ruth 4:9-10
nt: Eph 1:7; Gal 4:4-5; Titus 2:14
Boaz, a near kinsman, paid the price to redeem what was lost and took Ruth, a Gentile, as his bride. Christ became our kinsman, taking our nature, to redeem us.

== The LORD's anointed king
kind: prophecy
refs: 1Sam 2:10
nt: Luke 1:68-69
Hannah's song ends: the LORD shall give strength unto his king and exalt the horn of his anointed (his Messiah). Zacharias sings that God has raised up "an horn of salvation" in David's house.

== David defeats Goliath
kind: type
refs: 1Sam 17:45-47,50-51
nt: Col 2:15; Heb 2:14-15
The shepherd from Bethlehem faced the enemy his people feared, alone, and won the victory they all shared. Traditionally a picture of Christ's victory over Satan.

== David's son, a throne for ever
kind: prophecy
refs: 2Sam 7:12-16; 1Chr 17:11-14
nt: Luke 1:31-33; Acts 2:30; Heb 1:5; Rom 1:3
God promised David a son whose kingdom and throne would last for ever, and "I will be his father, and he shall be my son". Fulfilled in Jesus, the Son of David and Son of God.

== A greater than Solomon
kind: type
refs: 1Kgs 10:1,6-9
nt: Matt 12:42; Col 2:3
The queen of Sheba came from far away to hear Solomon's wisdom. Jesus said, "a greater than Solomon is here", in whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom.

== I know that my redeemer liveth
kind: prophecy
refs: Job 19:25-27
nt: 1Cor 15:20-23; 1John 3:2; Phil 3:20-21
In his suffering Job is sure his Redeemer lives and will stand on the earth at the last, and that he will see God in his flesh. Fulfilled in the risen Christ and the resurrection of believers.

== Joshua leads into the land
kind: type
refs: Josh 1:2-6
nt: Heb 4:8-10; Matt 1:21
Joshua (the same name as Jesus) led the people into the promised rest that Moses could not. Hebrews points beyond him to the rest that Jesus gives.

== The captain of the LORD's host
kind: type
refs: Josh 5:13-15
nt: Heb 2:10; Rev 19:11-14
Before Jericho, Joshua met a man with a drawn sword, the captain of the LORD's host, and worshipped him on holy ground. Traditionally an appearance of Christ, the captain of our salvation.

== A faithful priest
kind: prophecy
refs: 1Sam 2:35
nt: Heb 2:17; Heb 3:1-2; Heb 7:26-28
"I will raise me up a faithful priest, that shall do according to that which is in mine heart." Hebrews calls Jesus the merciful and faithful high priest.
