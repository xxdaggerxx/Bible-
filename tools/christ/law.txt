# Genesis to Deuteronomy

== The first promise of the gospel
kind: prophecy
refs: Gen 3:15
nt: Gal 4:4; Rom 16:20; Heb 2:14; 1John 3:8
The woman's offspring would crush the serpent's head, though bruised himself. Christ, born of a woman, defeated Satan by his death on the cross.

== Covered by a sacrifice
kind: type
refs: Gen 3:21
nt: Rom 3:22; Gal 3:27; Rev 3:18
God himself covered Adam and Eve's shame with skins, which cost a death. So Christ's righteousness, won by his death, covers the sinner.

== Adam, a figure of him to come
kind: type
refs: Gen 2:7
nt: Rom 5:14-19; 1Cor 15:45-49
Paul calls Adam "the figure of him that was to come". Through the first Adam came sin and death; through Christ, the last Adam, righteousness and life.

== The bridegroom and his bride
kind: type
refs: Gen 2:22-24
nt: Eph 5:25-32; Rev 19:7
The first marriage, the bride taken from the man's side, is "a great mystery" that Paul says speaks of Christ and the church.

== Abel's accepted lamb
kind: type
refs: Gen 4:4
nt: Heb 11:4; Heb 12:24; John 1:29
Abel brought a lamb from his flock and God accepted it. Christ is the Lamb of God, whose blood "speaketh better things than that of Abel".

== Saved in the ark
kind: type
refs: Gen 6:14; Gen 7:1,16
nt: 1Pet 3:20-21; Matt 24:37-39; John 10:9
Noah's family were brought safely through the judgement of the flood inside the ark, with its one door. So believers are saved from judgement in Christ.

== All nations blessed in Abraham
kind: prophecy
refs: Gen 12:3; Gen 18:18
nt: Gal 3:8; Acts 3:25-26
God promised that in Abraham all the families of the earth would be blessed. Paul calls this the gospel preached beforehand, fulfilled as the nations are blessed in Christ.

== Melchizedek, priest and king
kind: type
refs: Gen 14:18-20
nt: Heb 5:6-10; Heb 7:1-17
King of righteousness and of peace, bringing bread and wine, priest of the most high God with no recorded beginning or end. Hebrews says Christ is a priest for ever in his order.

== Abraham offers his only son
kind: type
refs: Gen 22:2,6-8,12-14
nt: John 1:29; John 3:16; Rom 8:32; Heb 11:17-19
A father offers his beloved only son on Moriah; the son carries the wood up the hill; "God will provide himself a lamb"; a ram dies in his place. A picture of the Father giving his Son.

== Abraham's seed
kind: prophecy
refs: Gen 22:18; Gen 26:4; Gen 28:14
nt: Gal 3:16; Acts 3:25-26
"In thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed." Paul points out that it says seed, not seeds: "which is Christ".

== Jacob's ladder
kind: type
refs: Gen 28:12-13
nt: John 1:51
Jacob saw a ladder joining earth to heaven, with angels going up and down. Jesus said this is the Son of Man: he is the way between heaven and earth.

== Joseph, rejected and then the saviour
kind: type
refs: Gen 37:3-4,26-28; Gen 45:4-7; Gen 50:20
nt: Acts 7:9-14; Acts 2:23-24; Acts 3:13-15
Loved by his father, hated and sold by his brothers for silver, Joseph was raised to the right hand of the king and saved the brothers who betrayed him. A picture of Christ.

== The sceptre of Judah
kind: prophecy
refs: Gen 49:10
nt: Heb 7:14; Rev 5:5; Luke 1:32-33
The ruler's sceptre would not leave Judah "until Shiloh come", and the peoples would gather to him. Christ came from Judah, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, King for ever.

== Moses the deliverer, refused
kind: type
refs: Exod 2:14; Exod 3:10
nt: Acts 7:35-37; Heb 3:1-6
Moses was first refused by his own people ("Who made thee a prince?") and then sent by God to deliver them. Stephen says Christ was rejected in the same way, and is the greater deliverer.

== The Passover lamb
kind: type
refs: Exod 12:3,5-7,13,46; Num 9:12
nt: 1Cor 5:7; John 1:29; John 19:36; 1Pet 1:18-19
A lamb without blemish was killed and its blood put on the door, and death passed over that house; not a bone of it was to be broken. "Christ our passover is sacrificed for us."

== Manna, bread from heaven
kind: type
refs: Exod 16:4,14-15
nt: John 6:31-35,48-51
God fed Israel in the wilderness with bread from heaven. Jesus said: "I am the bread of life... the living bread which came down from heaven."

== The smitten rock
kind: type
refs: Exod 17:6; Num 20:11
nt: 1Cor 10:4; John 7:37-39
Water flowed from the rock when it was struck, and the people drank. Paul says plainly, "that Rock was Christ"; struck once, he gives living water.

== God dwelling among his people
kind: type
refs: Exod 25:8-9; Exod 40:34
nt: John 1:14; Heb 9:11; Col 2:9
The tabernacle was God's tent among Israel, filled with his glory. "The Word was made flesh, and dwelt (tabernacled) among us, and we beheld his glory."

== The mercy seat
kind: type
refs: Exod 25:17-22
nt: Rom 3:25; Heb 9:5; 1John 2:2
On the lid of the ark the blood of atonement was sprinkled and God met with his people. Paul calls Christ the propitiation, the same word as mercy seat.

== The veil before the holiest
kind: type
refs: Exod 26:31-33
nt: Heb 10:19-20; Matt 27:51
The veil shut the way into God's presence. When Christ died it was torn from top to bottom; Hebrews says the veil is his flesh, the new and living way.

== The high priest
kind: type
refs: Exod 28:1,29,36-38
nt: Heb 4:14-16; Heb 7:24-27; Heb 9:11-12
Aaron bore the names of the tribes on his heart before God and bore the iniquity of their gifts. Christ is the great high priest who lives for ever to intercede for his people.

== The burnt offering
kind: type
refs: Lev 1:3-4,9
nt: Eph 5:2; Heb 10:5-10
A male without blemish, wholly given up to God, a sweet savour, accepted for the one who brought it. Christ "hath given himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour".

== The sin offering, burned outside the camp
kind: type
refs: Lev 4:12,29; Lev 16:27
nt: 2Cor 5:21; Heb 13:11-12
The sinner laid his hand on the animal, which died for his sin, and its body was burned outside the camp. So Jesus, made sin for us, "suffered without the gate".

== Life is in the blood
kind: type
refs: Lev 17:11
nt: Heb 9:22; Eph 1:7; 1John 1:7
God gave the blood on the altar to make atonement for souls. "Without shedding of blood is no remission": the blood of Christ cleanses from all sin.

== The Day of Atonement and the scapegoat
kind: type
refs: Lev 16:15-16,21-22,30
nt: Heb 9:7-12,24-28; John 1:29
Once a year the high priest took blood into the holiest place, and a goat carried the people's sins away into the wilderness. Christ entered heaven once for all with his own blood and took away sin.

== Firstfruits
kind: type
refs: Lev 23:10-11
nt: 1Cor 15:20-23
The first sheaf of the harvest was waved before the LORD the day after the sabbath. Christ rose on that day, "the firstfruits of them that slept", the pledge of the harvest to come.

== The red heifer
kind: type
refs: Num 19:2-4,9
nt: Heb 9:13-14; Heb 13:12
A heifer without spot, slain outside the camp, whose ashes cleansed the unclean. Hebrews: how much more shall the blood of Christ purge your conscience.

== The bronze serpent
kind: type
refs: Num 21:8-9
nt: John 3:14-15
Whoever looked at the serpent lifted up on the pole lived. Jesus: "As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up."

== A Star out of Jacob
kind: prophecy
refs: Num 24:17
nt: Matt 2:2; Rev 22:16; Luke 1:78-79
Balaam saw a Star come out of Jacob and a Sceptre rise out of Israel. Christ is "the bright and morning star", the King whose star the wise men followed.

== The city of refuge
kind: type
refs: Num 35:11-12,25
nt: Heb 6:18-20
The one who fled to a city of refuge was safe from the avenger, until the high priest died. Believers "have fled for refuge" to Christ, the high priest who lives for ever.

== A prophet like Moses
kind: prophecy
refs: Deut 18:15,18-19
nt: Acts 3:22-26; Acts 7:37; John 6:14; John 1:45
God promised to raise up a prophet like Moses, with his words in his mouth, whom all must hear. Peter and Stephen preached that this prophet is Jesus.

== Hanged on a tree
kind: type
refs: Deut 21:23
nt: Gal 3:13; Acts 5:30; 1Pet 2:24
"He that is hanged is accursed of God." Paul quotes this of the cross: Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us.

== The bread and the lampstand
kind: type
refs: Exod 25:30-31,37
nt: John 6:35; John 8:12; Rev 1:12-13
The bread of the presence lay always before the LORD, and the golden lampstand gave the only light in the holy place. Jesus: "I am the bread of life" and "I am the light of the world."

== The altar of sacrifice
kind: type
refs: Exod 27:1-2
nt: Heb 13:10-12; Heb 10:10-14
At the bronze altar, outside the tent, every sacrifice was offered; no one came to God without passing it. "We have an altar": the cross where Christ offered himself once for all.

== The lamb morning and evening
kind: type
refs: Exod 29:38-42
nt: John 1:29; Heb 7:27; Heb 10:11-12
Every day a lamb was offered in the morning and another in the evening, without end. Christ, the Lamb of God, offered one sacrifice for sins for ever.

== The altar of incense
kind: type
refs: Exod 30:1,6-8
nt: Heb 7:25; Rom 8:34; Rev 8:3-4
Incense burned before the veil morning and evening, a picture of prayer rising to God. Christ "ever liveth to make intercession" for his people.

== The peace offering
kind: type
refs: Lev 3:1-2,5
nt: Eph 2:13-17; Col 1:20
A sacrifice shared with God and the worshipper in fellowship. Christ "is our peace", having made peace through the blood of his cross.

== The cleansing of the leper
kind: type
refs: Lev 14:4-7
nt: Mark 1:40-42; Heb 9:13-14; Heb 10:22
One bird was killed over running water, and the living bird, dipped in its blood, was let go: the leper was clean. A picture of Christ's death and resurrection cleansing the sinner.

== The year of jubilee
kind: type
refs: Lev 25:9-10
nt: Luke 4:18-21; Gal 5:1
On the Day of Atonement the trumpet sounded liberty throughout the land; debts were cancelled and the captive went home. Jesus came "to preach deliverance to the captives... the acceptable year of the Lord".

== Aaron's rod that budded
kind: type
refs: Num 17:8,10
nt: Heb 9:4; Heb 5:4-6; Rom 1:4
A dead rod brought forth buds, blossoms and almonds overnight, showing whom God had chosen as priest. Traditionally a picture of Christ, the chosen priest, raised to life.
