# Psalms and Proverbs

== The nations against the LORD's anointed
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 2:1-2
nt: Acts 4:25-28
The kings of the earth set themselves against the LORD and his anointed. The apostles prayed that this was fulfilled when Herod, Pilate, the Gentiles and Israel gathered against Jesus.

== Thou art my Son
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 2:7-9,12
nt: Acts 13:33; Heb 1:5; Heb 5:5; Matt 3:17; Rev 2:27; Rev 19:15
"Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee." The New Testament applies this to Jesus, declared the Son of God with power by his resurrection, who will rule the nations.

== Out of the mouth of babes
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 8:2
nt: Matt 21:15-16
When the children in the temple cried "Hosanna to the Son of David", Jesus answered with this verse: God has perfected praise out of the mouths of babes.

== Crowned with glory, all things under his feet
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 8:4-6
nt: Heb 2:6-9; 1Cor 15:27; Eph 1:22
Man made a little lower than the angels and crowned with glory. Hebrews: "we see Jesus", who for the suffering of death is crowned with glory, with all things put under him.

== Not left in the grave
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 16:8-11
nt: Acts 2:25-32; Acts 13:35-37
"Thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption." Peter and Paul preach that David spoke of the resurrection of Christ.

== My God, why hast thou forsaken me?
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 22:1
nt: Matt 27:46; Mark 15:34
Jesus cried the first words of this psalm from the cross. The whole psalm, written a thousand years before, describes the crucifixion.

== Mocked by those who watched
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 22:6-8
nt: Matt 27:39-44; Luke 23:35
Despised by the people, they shake the head: "He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him." The rulers used almost these words as they mocked Jesus on the cross.

== They pierced my hands and my feet
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 22:14-17
nt: John 19:28; John 20:25-27; Luke 24:39-40
Poured out like water, bones out of joint, tongue cleaving to the jaws, hands and feet pierced. A picture of crucifixion, a death not yet known when it was written.

== They part my garments
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 22:18
nt: John 19:23-24; Matt 27:35
"They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture." The soldiers did exactly this at the foot of the cross.

== All the ends of the world shall turn
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 22:27-31
nt: Rev 7:9-10; John 19:30; Acts 13:47
The psalm of the cross ends in triumph: all the nations turn to the LORD, and his righteousness is declared, "that he hath done this". Fulfilled as the gospel goes to all nations.

== I will declare thy name unto my brethren
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 22:22
nt: Heb 2:11-12
After the suffering, praise. Hebrews puts these words in Christ's mouth: he is not ashamed to call his people brethren.

== The LORD is my shepherd
kind: type
refs: Ps 23:1
nt: John 10:11,14; Heb 13:20; 1Pet 2:25
The LORD is the shepherd of his people. Jesus says, "I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep."

== The King of glory
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 24:7-10
nt: 1Cor 2:8; Eph 4:8-10
"Lift up your heads, O ye gates; and the King of glory shall come in." Traditionally sung of Christ's ascension; Paul calls Jesus "the Lord of glory".

== Into thine hand I commit my spirit
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 31:5
nt: Luke 23:46
Jesus' last words on the cross were these: "Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit."

== Not one of his bones broken
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 34:20
nt: John 19:32-36
The soldiers broke the legs of the two thieves but not of Jesus, who was already dead, "that the scripture should be fulfilled, A bone of him shall not be broken."

== False witnesses and hatred without a cause
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 35:11,19; Ps 69:4
nt: Matt 26:59-61; John 15:25
False witnesses rose up; "they hated me without a cause". Jesus said this word was fulfilled in him.

== Lo, I come to do thy will
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 40:6-8
nt: Heb 10:5-10
Sacrifices God did not desire, but "Lo, I come... I delight to do thy will". Hebrews says Christ spoke these words coming into the world, and by that will we are sanctified through his body offered once for all.

== Betrayed by a close friend
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 41:9
nt: John 13:18,21-27; Matt 26:23
"Mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted, which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me." Jesus quoted this of Judas at the Last Supper.

== Thy throne, O God, is for ever
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 45:6-7
nt: Heb 1:8-9
The royal psalm calls the king God, anointed with the oil of gladness above his fellows. Hebrews says God says this "unto the Son".

== Let all the gods worship him
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 97:7
nt: Heb 1:6
"Worship him, all ye gods." Hebrews applies this to the Son: "when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, he saith, And let all the angels of God worship him."

== Ascended on high
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 68:18
nt: Eph 4:8-10
"Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive: thou hast received gifts for men." Paul applies it to Christ's ascension and the gifts he gives his church.

== Zeal for thy house
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 69:8-9
nt: John 2:17; John 7:5; Rom 15:3
A stranger to his brothers, eaten up with zeal for God's house, bearing the reproaches meant for God. The disciples remembered this when Jesus cleared the temple.

== Vinegar to drink
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 69:21
nt: Matt 27:34,48; John 19:28-30
"In my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink." On the cross Jesus said "I thirst", and was given vinegar.

== Another shall take his office
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 69:25; Ps 109:8
nt: Acts 1:16-20
Peter quoted these verses of Judas, who betrayed Jesus, when the apostles chose another to take his place.

== Kings bring gifts
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 72:10-11,17
nt: Matt 2:1-11; Rev 21:24
Kings shall bring presents and all nations shall serve him and call him blessed. The wise men from the east brought gifts to the infant Christ.

== Speaking in parables
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 78:2
nt: Matt 13:34-35
"I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old." Matthew says Jesus taught in parables to fulfil this.

== David's seed for ever
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 89:3-4,27-29,35-36
nt: Luke 1:32-33; Acts 2:30; Rev 1:5
God swore that David's seed would endure for ever and his throne as the days of heaven; "I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth". Fulfilled in Christ.

== Thou art the same
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 102:25-27
nt: Heb 1:10-12; Heb 13:8
The heavens will perish, "but thou art the same, and thy years shall have no end". Hebrews says this is spoken to the Son, the Creator.

== Sit thou at my right hand
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 110:1-2
nt: Matt 22:41-45; Acts 2:34-36; Heb 1:13; Heb 10:12-13
"The LORD said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand." Jesus asked how David's son could be David's Lord; the apostles preach him seated at God's right hand. The verse the New Testament quotes most.

== A priest for ever
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 110:4
nt: Heb 5:6; Heb 7:17-25
"Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek." Hebrews builds on this oath: Christ, King and Priest, lives for ever to make intercession.

== The rejected stone
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 118:22-23
nt: Matt 21:42; Acts 4:10-12; 1Pet 2:7
"The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner." Jesus applied it to himself, and Peter preached it of his rejection and resurrection.

== Blessed is he that cometh
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 118:26
nt: Matt 21:9; Matt 23:39; John 12:13
The crowds sang this psalm as Jesus rode into Jerusalem: "Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord."

== Of the fruit of thy body upon thy throne
kind: prophecy
refs: Ps 132:11,17
nt: Acts 2:30; Luke 1:69
God swore to David that of the fruit of his body he would set one on his throne. Peter says David, a prophet, knew this and spoke of the resurrection of Christ.

== Wisdom with God at creation
kind: type
refs: Prov 8:22-31
nt: John 1:1-3; Col 1:15-17; 1Cor 1:24,30
Wisdom was with the LORD before the world, beside him as he made all things, his daily delight. Traditionally read of Christ, "the wisdom of God", by whom all things were made.
