# Bible numbers: marked only where the Bible gives a number meaning (traditional readings only;
# no letter-value codes or date-setting).

== One
kind: number
forms: one, one lord, one god, one body, one spirit, one flesh
refs: Gen 2:24; Deut 6:4; Mark 10:8; Mark 12:29,32; John 10:16,30; John 17:11,21-23; Rom 12:5; 1Cor 8:6; 1Cor 10:17; 1Cor 12:12-13,20; Gal 3:28; Eph 2:14-16; Eph 4:4-6; 1Tim 2:5
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Unity and the uniqueness of God: "the Lord our God is one Lord" (Deut 6:4). Jesus prayed that his people "may be one", and Paul writes of "one body, and one Spirit... one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all" (Eph 4:4-6).

== Two
kind: number
forms: two, twain, two or three, two witnesses
refs: Deut 17:6; Deut 19:15; Eccl 4:9-12; Matt 18:16,19-20; Mark 6:7; Luke 10:1; John 8:17; 2Cor 13:1; 1Tim 5:19; Heb 10:28; Rev 11:3
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Witness and confirmation: "at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established" (Deut 19:15). Jesus sent his disciples out two by two.

== Three
kind: number
forms: three, third, thrice, three days, third day, holy holy holy
refs: Isa 6:3; Hos 6:2; Jonah 1:17; Matt 12:40; Matt 16:21; Matt 17:23; Matt 20:19; Matt 26:34,61,75; Matt 27:40,63; Matt 28:19; Mark 8:31; Mark 9:31; Mark 10:34; Luke 9:22; Luke 24:7,21,46; John 2:19-20; John 21:17; Acts 10:40; 1Cor 15:4; 2Cor 13:14; Rev 4:8
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Completeness and the work of God; above all, the third day of Christ's resurrection: "he was buried, and... rose again the third day according to the scriptures" (1 Cor 15:4). The threefold "Holy, holy, holy" and the threefold name of Father, Son and Holy Spirit have long been read as pointing to the Trinity.

== Four
kind: number
forms: four, fourth, four corners, four winds, four living creatures, four beasts
refs: Isa 11:12; Ezek 1:5-10; Ezek 37:9; Dan 7:2-3,17; Zech 1:18-21; Zech 6:1-5; Matt 24:31; Rev 4:6-8; Rev 5:6-14; Rev 6:1-8; Rev 7:1-2; Rev 9:14-15; Rev 20:8
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
The whole created world, with its four directions: the four winds, the four corners of the earth, the four living creatures around the throne and the four horsemen sent through the earth.

== Six and 666
kind: number
forms: six, six hundred threescore and six, 666, six hundred sixty six, six hundred and sixty six, threescore, sixty
refs: Dan 3:1; Rev 13:18
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
"Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six" (Rev 13:18). Because six falls short of seven, the number of completeness, the traditional reading is that it marks the beast as a human counterfeit that always falls short of God. Many have tried to name a person from it; the Bible does not say who it is. Nebuchadnezzar's idol was sixty cubits high and six wide.

== Seven
kind: number
forms: seven, seventh, sevenfold, seven times, sevens, seventy times seven, seven spirits, seven churches, seven seals, seven trumpets, seven vials, seven bowls, seven stars, seven lamps, seven eyes, seven horns
refs: Gen 2:2-3; Gen 4:24; Gen 7:2-4; Exod 20:10; Lev 4:6,17; Lev 16:14,19; Lev 23:15-16; Lev 25:3-4,8; Josh 6:4,8,13,15; 2Kgs 5:10,14; Ps 12:6; Ps 119:164; Prov 9:1; Prov 24:16; Zech 3:9; Zech 4:2,10; Matt 18:21-22; Luke 17:4; Heb 4:4; Rev 1:4,11-13,16,20; Rev 2:1; Rev 3:1; Rev 4:5; Rev 5:1,5-6; Rev 8:2,6; Rev 10:3-4; Rev 11:13; Rev 12:3; Rev 13:1; Rev 15:1,6-8; Rev 16:1; Rev 17:1,3,7,9-11; Rev 21:9
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Completeness and perfection, especially God's own work, from the seven days of creation and the seventh-day Sabbath. Sevens run through the Law (sprinkling seven times), the fall of Jericho, and above all Revelation: seven churches, seals, trumpets and bowls, and the Lamb's seven horns and eyes (perfect power and knowledge). Jesus' "seventy times seven" means forgiving without limit.

== Eight
kind: number
forms: eight, eighth, eighth day, eight souls
refs: Gen 17:12; Lev 12:3; Lev 23:36,39; Luke 2:21; John 20:26; 1Pet 3:20; 2Pet 2:5
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
A new beginning, one beyond the complete seven: boys were circumcised on the eighth day, eight people came through the flood into a new world, and Jesus rose on the first day of a new week. This reading is traditional but less certain than for seven or twelve.

== Ten
kind: number
forms: ten, tenth, ten commandments, ten horns, ten days, ten virgins, ten talents, ten pieces of silver, ten servants, ten pounds
refs: Exod 34:28; Deut 4:13; Deut 10:4; Dan 1:12-20; Dan 7:7,20,24; Matt 25:1,28; Luke 15:8; Luke 19:13-17; Rev 2:10; Rev 12:3; Rev 13:1; Rev 17:3,7,12,16
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
A complete or full number, as of the fingers on two hands: the Ten Commandments, the ten plagues, ten virgins in the parable. In Daniel and Revelation ten horns are ten kings, the full sum of worldly power.

== Twelve
kind: number
forms: twelve, twelfth, twelve tribes, twelve apostles, twelve stars, twelve gates, twelve foundations, twelve thrones, twelve disciples
refs: Gen 35:22; Gen 49:28; Exod 24:4; Exod 28:21; Josh 4:2-9; 1Kgs 18:31; Matt 10:1-5; Matt 19:28; Mark 3:14; Luke 22:30; Acts 26:7; Jas 1:1; Rev 7:5-8; Rev 12:1; Rev 21:12-21; Rev 22:2
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
God's people, complete: the twelve tribes of Israel and the twelve apostles of the Lamb. The new Jerusalem has twelve gates with the tribes' names and twelve foundations with the apostles' names: Old and New Testament people of God together.

== Thirty pieces of silver
kind: number
forms: thirty, thirty pieces of silver, thirty shekels, thirty silver coins
refs: Exod 21:32; Zech 11:12-13; Matt 26:15; Matt 27:3-10
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
The price of a slave under the Law (Exod 21:32). Zechariah was paid thirty pieces of silver as an insult and threw them to the potter; Judas betrayed Jesus for the same sum, and it was used to buy the potter's field.

== Forty
kind: number
forms: forty, fortieth, forty days, forty years, forty days and forty nights
refs: Gen 7:4,12,17; Exod 24:18; Exod 34:28; Num 13:25; Num 14:33-34; Deut 8:2,4; Deut 9:9,11,18,25; Deut 29:5; Judg 3:11; Judg 5:31; Judg 8:28; 1Sam 17:16; 1Kgs 19:8; Ezek 4:6; Jonah 3:4; Matt 4:2; Mark 1:13; Luke 4:2; Acts 1:3; Acts 7:30,36; Acts 13:18; Heb 3:9,17
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
A time of testing, trial and preparation: forty days of rain in the flood, Moses forty days on Sinai, Israel forty years in the wilderness, Elijah forty days to Horeb, and Jesus forty days tempted in the wilderness. Jesus appeared to his disciples for forty days after he rose.

== Fifty
kind: number
forms: fifty, fiftieth, fifty days
refs: Lev 23:16; Lev 25:10-11; Num 4:3; Acts 2:1
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Freedom and fulfilment: the fiftieth year was the Jubilee, when liberty was proclaimed, and the fiftieth day after Passover was Pentecost, when the Holy Spirit was given.

== Seventy
kind: number
forms: seventy, threescore and ten, seventy elders, seventy years, seventy two, seventy-two, other seventy
refs: Gen 46:27; Exod 1:5; Exod 24:1,9; Num 11:16,24-25; Ps 90:10; Jer 25:11-12; Jer 29:10; Dan 9:2; Zech 1:12; Zech 7:5; Luke 10:1,17
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
A full number of people or years: seventy elders shared Moses' work, the exile in Babylon lasted seventy years as Jeremiah foretold, and Jesus sent out seventy (some manuscripts, seventy-two) to go ahead of him, often linked with the seventy nations of Genesis 10.

== Seventy weeks
kind: number
forms: seventy weeks, seventy sevens, weeks, sevens, threescore and two weeks, sixty-two weeks, one week
refs: Dan 9:24-27
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Gabriel told Daniel that "seventy weeks" (seventy sevens, of years) were decreed "to make an end of sins... and to bring in everlasting righteousness" and to anoint the most Holy. Traditional Protestant interpreters count the weeks of years from the decree to rebuild Jerusalem to the coming and death of the Messiah, Christ, "cut off, but not for himself" (Dan 9:26).

== Three and a half (a time, times and half a time)
kind: number
forms: time and times and the dividing of time, time and times and half a time, a time and times and half a time, forty and two months, forty-two months, 42 months, thousand two hundred and threescore days, 1 260 days, 1260 days, three days and an half, three and a half days, months, days, time
refs: Dan 7:25; Dan 12:7,11-12; Rev 11:2-3,9,11; Rev 12:6,14; Rev 13:5
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Half of seven: a broken, limited time of trouble that God has set and will cut short. It appears as "a time, and times, and half a time", 42 months and 1,260 days: the time the holy city is trampled, the woman is kept safe in the wilderness and the beast is allowed to act. However hard the time, it is limited, and it ends at God's appointed time.

== 144,000
kind: number
forms: 144, hundred and forty and four thousand, hundred forty-four thousand, one hundred and forty-four thousand, 144 000, one hundred forty-four thousand
refs: Rev 7:4-8; Rev 14:1-5
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
Twelve times twelve times a thousand: God's people complete, every one sealed and kept safe, none missing. Revelation 7 lists twelve thousand from each tribe, then John sees "a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations" (Rev 7:9). Many traditional interpreters see the two as the same people of God seen two ways.

== A thousand years
kind: number
forms: thousand years, thousand, a thousand years, 1 000 years, 1000 years
refs: Ps 50:10; Ps 90:4; 2Pet 3:8; Rev 20:2-7
sources: Easton's Bible Dictionary
A thousand stands for a great, complete number ("the cattle upon a thousand hills", Ps 50:10), and "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years" (2 Pet 3:8). In Revelation 20 Satan is bound for a thousand years while the saints reign with Christ; Augustine and the Reformers read this as Christ's reign now, from his resurrection to his return, with Satan restrained so the gospel goes to the nations.
