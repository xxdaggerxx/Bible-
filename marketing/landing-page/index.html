Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Ink & Word is a non-profit project. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy inside the app.

Does it need the internet?

No. The built-in Bibles, commentaries, reflections and study tools all work offline. Online Bibles such as the NIV download each chapter the first time you read it, then keep it for offline use.

Which Bible versions are there?

The King James Version, Berean Standard Bible and World English Bible are built in. More versions, such as the NIV, can be added online. You can also import a Bible you have the right to use.

Do I need a pen?

No. A pen like Apple Pencil or the S Pen gives the best writing experience on a tablet, but you can draw with your finger on any tablet or phone, and with the mouse on a computer.

Which devices does it run on?

iPad and iPhone, Android tablets and phones, and Windows and Mac computers (Linux too). It's the same app everywhere, with the same Bibles, study tools and notes. On a computer, bring your notes across with a backup file; sync with Google Drive there is coming.

Where do my notes go?

They stay on your device. If you turn on sync, they're also kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive, so your phone and tablet match. Nobody else can see them. You can also back everything up to one file and restore it on another device.

How was the Ink & Word Commentary written?

It was summarised by computer from the classic commentaries in the app, such as Matthew Henry, Barnes and Clarke, and from trusted Christian websites, then checked so that every point names a source that really says it. It wasn't written by one person, so check anything important against the other commentaries and Scripture.
